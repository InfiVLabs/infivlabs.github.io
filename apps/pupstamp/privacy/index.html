Pupstamp privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026. Pupstamp is published by InfiV Labs ("we"). Contact: infivlabs@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your dogs' names, photos, ages, goals, training sessions, notes, potty log, socialisation checklist and cue sheet are stored only on your device. We have no server and no account system, and we never receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it. A backup file is created only when you choose "Export backup", and it goes wherever you send it.

What third-party services receive

  • Google AdMob (only in markets where the app shows ads, and only after consent where the law requires it): device advertising identifier, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions. Used to show and measure ads. Google privacy policy.
  • Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics: app usage events (for example "session completed") without your dog's details, device model, OS version and crash reports. Used to understand use and fix crashes. Ad personalisation signals are switched off by default.
  • RevenueCat and Google Play Billing: purchase and subscription status, and an anonymous app user ID. Used to unlock Pupstamp Plus and restore purchases. RevenueCat privacy policy.

Permissions

Notifications: only if you turn on the daily reminder. Photos: through the system photo picker, only the photo you choose. Vibration: for clicker feedback.

Your choices

You can reopen the ad consent form in Settings → Privacy options (where available), turn reminders off, reset your advertising ID in your device settings, and delete all app data by uninstalling. Children: Pupstamp is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when the app's data use changes.
