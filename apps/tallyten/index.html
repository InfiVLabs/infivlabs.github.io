Tally Ten: the number pairs puzzle where you make ten

Pair equal numbers or make ten. Every Journey board can be cleared by logic.

Tally Ten is a number puzzle game based on the old pencil game Take Ten. You strike out two numbers that are equal, like 4 and 4, or that add up to ten, like 3 and 7, until the page is clear. When you run out of pairs, the numbers still open are written again at the bottom. Every Journey and Daily board has been checked by a solver before you get it, so it can always be cleared by logic, never luck.

How it plays

  • Tap two equal numbers, or two that make ten.
  • Pairs can sit side by side, one above the other, on a diagonal, or across the end of a line, and struck-out numbers never block them.
  • Out of moves? Use an add to write the open numbers again at the bottom. Each board gives you only a few, and Expert boards none.
  • A row you empty disappears, and emptying rows close together builds a combo.

The full rules, with a worked example board, are in how to play the number matching game.

Four ways to play

  • Journey: 300 boards climbing from Easy through Medium and Hard to Expert and Extreme, with up to three ticks each.
  • Today's sheet: one Daily board for everyone, gentle on Monday and toughest on Sunday. Missed days stay open in the calendar, and a full month earns a trophy.
  • Pen Book: 100 harder pages where many pairs quietly ruin the page.
  • Classic: the endless pencil game, stage after stage, with fewer adds as you go.

Who it is for

Tally Ten is a number game for adults who like thinking one move ahead. You need to know only that 3 and 7 make ten. Hard and Expert are hard because of fewer adds and more pairs that lead nowhere, not because of a clock.

It is also easy on the eyes. Big Numbers lays the board out in six columns with digits twice the size, and in dark mode the exercise-book page turns into a chalkboard. The guide to number games for seniors has more on choosing and setting up readable puzzles.

How it differs

On Easy and Medium boards, a tick shows while a clear is still possible. If a move loses it, a red-pen note in the margin names that move and takes you back to it. Hints come from the same solver: they point to a pair that keeps the clear alive, or tell you it is time to add. Paper rules switch off diagonals for the original rows-and-columns game. For tactics, see the strategy guide and cheat sheet.

Questions

Is Tally Ten free?

Yes, every mode and every difficulty is free, with three hints a day plus extra hints on most boards. The free version shows ads: a banner on the contents page, an occasional full-screen ad between boards, and optional short ads for an extra hint or add. Remove Ads is a single purchase. An optional VIP subscription removes the daily hint limit and the ads.

Does Tally Ten work offline?

Yes. It plays offline with no account, and your progress stays on your phone.

Can I make the numbers bigger?

Yes. Choose Big Numbers when you start, or switch it on later in settings: six columns and digits twice the size.

What is the game called on paper?

The pencil version is usually called Take Ten. Its history and paper rules are in the game where you match numbers to make 10.
