How to sign a PDF on iPhone

How to sign a PDF on iPhone with the built-in Markup tool in Files, Mail or Preview: create your signature once, place it, and send the signed copy.

Updated 26 Nov 20265 min read

To sign a PDF on iPhone, open it in the Files app, tap the Markup button (the pen tip), tap the Add button (+), choose Add Signature, sign with your finger, then drag the signature onto the line and tap Done. The iPhone saves your signature, so next time you only pick it from the list. No extra app or account is needed.

Before you start: get the PDF into Files

Markup works on a PDF you can open in Files, Mail or, on iOS 26 and later, the Preview app. If the PDF arrived somewhere else:

  • From an email in Apple Mail: you can also sign it while you write the reply (see below).
  • From Gmail, WhatsApp or a website: open the PDF, tap the Share button and choose Save to Files.

Keep the unsigned version. In Files, touch and hold the PDF and tap Duplicate, then sign the copy. Markup in Files changes the file itself, so a duplicate saves you from asking for the document again if something goes wrong.

Sign a PDF in the Files app

  1. Open the PDF in Files.
  2. Tap the Markup button.
  3. Tap the Add button (+), then Add Signature.
  4. The first time, sign with your finger on the signing screen. Tap Clear to try again, then Done.
  5. The signature lands on the page. Drag it onto the signature line and drag a blue corner dot to resize it.
  6. Tap Done to save.

Your saved signatures stay available for future documents. To keep separate entries for your full signature and your initials, label each one as you create it: Apple's signing screen has a menu next to New Signature where you can pick a label such as initials.

Sign and send back an email attachment in Mail

Apple's Mail app can mark up a PDF while you write the reply:

  1. Save the attachment to Files first (tap it, tap Share, then Save to Files).
  2. Reply to the email. Tap inside the message body to show the format bar, tap the Attachment button and pick the PDF.
  3. Tap the attached PDF, tap the More button, then Markup.
  4. Tap Add (+), then Add Signature, and place it as above.
  5. Tap Done, then send the reply.

Sign with the Preview app (iOS 26 and later)

Recent versions of iOS include Preview, the same PDF app Mac users know. Open Preview, find the PDF under Browse, go to the page you want to sign, tap Markup, then Add and Add Signature. The steps after that match the Files app.

Filling in a form before you sign

Apple's guide for forms: open the form in Files, tap the AutoFill button, then tap a blank field and type. If iPhone suggests your details, check each field before you tap Fill. When the form is done, add your signature with Add and Add Signature, then use the menu to export, print or share it.

Sending the signed PDF back

  • In Mail, attach the signed copy to your reply.
  • From Files, tap the PDF, tap Share, and choose your mail app or a chat app. In chat apps, send it as a document so it stays a PDF.
  • Open the signed file once before sending. Check the signature sits on the right line and every page is there.

What Markup doesn't do well

Markup is fine for a single signature. It is slower for:

  • Initials on every page. You add them one page at a time. Our guide to initialing every page of a PDF has tips for keeping them in the same spot.
  • A company stamp. Markup is built for drawing and signatures, not for placing a cleaned-up stamp image. See how to add a stamp to a PDF.
  • Your pen signature. Markup signatures are drawn with a finger on glass, so they rarely look like the signature you make with a pen.

Common mistakes

  • Signing a page that isn't the signature page. Scroll through the whole document first; many contracts have signature and initial lines on more than one page.
  • A huge signature. Shrink it to the height of the line. A signature that covers printed text can make the page hard to read.
  • Sending the original. If you signed a duplicate, share the duplicate.
  • Forgetting the date. Many documents ask for the date next to the signature. Add it with the Add button's text option.

What kind of signature this is

A signature added with Markup is a simple electronic signature: a picture of your signature on the page. US law (the ESIGN Act) and EU rules (eIDAS) say a signature can't be refused legal effect only because it is electronic. That doesn't make it a certificate-based digital signature, and some documents, such as wills, need other forms of signing.

This is general information, not legal advice. If a document asks for a digital certificate, a notary or a wet signature, follow its instructions, and ask a lawyer if you are unsure.

Questions

How do I sign a PDF on my iPhone for free?

Open the PDF in Files, tap Markup, tap the Add button and choose Add Signature. Sign with your finger, place the signature, and tap Done. It is built into iOS and costs nothing.

How do I sign a document on iPhone without Adobe?

Use Markup in the Files app, in Mail, or in the Preview app on iOS 26 and later. All three can add your saved signature without any other app.

How do I sign a PDF from Gmail on my iPhone?

Open the attachment, tap Share and choose Save to Files. Sign it in Files with Markup, then attach the signed copy to your reply in Gmail.

Can I save my initials as well as my signature on iPhone?

Yes. Create a second signature with your initials and give it the initials label. Both appear in the list when you tap Add Signature.
