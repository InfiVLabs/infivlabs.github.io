How to initial every page of a PDF

How to initial every page of a PDF on your phone, what "initial every page" means, where initials go, and how to add them to all pages at once.

Updated 26 Nov 20265 min read

To initial every page of a PDF, add your initials in the same spot on each page, usually the bottom-right corner, and put your full signature only where the document asks for it. On a phone you can save your initials once and place them page by page, or use an app that copies them to every page in one step. Check the page count when you finish, so no page is missed.

What "initial every page" means

Initials are the first letters of your names: Alex Morgan initials "AM". When a contract says "initial every page", it wants that short mark on each page, not your full signature.

The usual reasons:

  • It shows you saw every page. Nobody can easily claim a page was skipped.
  • It makes swapping a page harder. A replacement page wouldn't carry your handwritten initials.
  • It ties loose pages together. Long leases, employment contracts and loan papers are often printed and stapled; initials on each sheet connect them.

Some documents also ask for initials next to a single clause, such as a cancellation term, or beside a handwritten change. Those are separate from initialing every page.

Where to put your initials

  • Bottom-right corner is the most common place, often on a short line marked "Initials".
  • Same place on every page. It looks deliberate and is easy to check.
  • Small. Around the height of a line of text is enough. Don't cover printed words or numbers.
  • The last page: if it holds the signature block, many documents want your full signature there and initials on the pages before it. If the document is unclear, ask the sender.

If two people sign, each initials every page. Leave room for the other person's initials next to yours.

Initial every page on iPhone

The iPhone's built-in Markup tool can store initials as a separate saved signature.

  1. Open the PDF in the Files app and tap the Markup button.
  2. Tap Add (+), then Add Signature. If you already have a signature, tap Add or Remove Signature, then the Add button.
  3. Write your initials. Use the menu next to New Signature to label it as initials, then tap Done.
  4. Drag the initials to the corner of page 1 and resize them.
  5. Scroll to the next page, tap Add, Add Signature, choose your initials, and place them in the same corner. Repeat for every page.
  6. Tap Done to save.

On a 20-page lease that is 20 rounds of the same taps, so take it slowly and count. Our guide on how to sign a PDF on iPhone covers keeping the unsigned original safe first.

Initial every page on Android

Google Drive's PDF annotate tool and Files by Google's Edit tool let you draw on the page, but they don't store initials. You write them by hand on each page:

  1. Open the PDF in Drive and tap the Annotate pen.
  2. Zoom in on the bottom-right corner of page 1 and write your initials.
  3. Scroll to page 2 and repeat, and so on to the last page.
  4. Save a copy under a new name.

Handwritten initials will vary a little from page to page, which is normal on paper too. See how to sign a PDF on Android for the full Drive, Files by Google and Samsung Notes steps.

Initial all pages at once

Some PDF signing apps can place a mark once and copy it to the same position on every page. That is the quickest way to handle long documents. Two things to check afterwards:

  • Pages with a different layout. A landscape page or a page with a table in the corner may need the initials moved by hand.
  • The signature page. If you don't want initials there, look for a "skip last page" option or remove them from that page.

Common mistakes

  • Initialing only the first and last pages. Every page means every page, including schedules, annexes and attached plans.
  • Using a different mark on some pages. Keep the same initials throughout.
  • Initialing a draft. If the document changes after you initial it, the new version needs initials again.
  • Covering text. A clause hidden under your initials can cause arguments later.
  • Forgetting to count. After saving, scroll through the whole PDF and check each page has your initials.

What kind of signature this is

Initials placed on a PDF are a simple electronic signature, the same as a signature image. US law (the ESIGN Act) and EU rules (eIDAS) don't refuse an electronic signature legal effect only because it is electronic, but some documents, such as wills, need other forms of signing.

This is general information, not legal advice. If a document asks for wet ink, a notary or a certificate-based signature, follow its instructions, and ask a lawyer if you are unsure.

Questions

What does initial every page mean?

It means writing your initials, the first letters of your names, on each page of the document, usually in the bottom-right corner. It shows you saw every page and makes it harder to swap one later.

Can I initial all pages of a PDF at once?

Not with the free built-in tools on iPhone or Android; they place initials one page at a time. Some PDF signing apps can copy your initials to every page in one step.

Where do you put initials on a contract?

In the bottom-right corner of each page, or on the line marked "Initials" if the document has one. Keep them in the same place on every page.

Do I initial the signature page too?

Many documents want a full signature on the last page and initials on the pages before it. If the document doesn't say, ask the person who sent it.
