How to add a stamp to a PDF

How to add a stamp to a PDF: turn your rubber company stamp into a clean image with a see-through background, place it, and stamp every page.

Updated 26 Nov 20265 min read

To add your company stamp to a PDF, stamp it once on plain white paper, photograph it straight from above, remove the white background so only the ink is left, and place that image on the page next to your signature. Save the result as a new signed copy. The background step matters most: a stamp with a white box around it covers the text and lines underneath and looks pasted on.

When a PDF needs a stamp

Many businesses stamp their own paperwork: invoices, quotations, delivery notes, purchase orders, letters and agreements. In some countries a company stamp next to the signature is the normal way to show the document comes from the business, not only from the person signing. If a client or supplier asks for "signature and stamp", this is what they mean.

Use only your own organization's stamp, and only on documents you are allowed to sign for. A stamp image on a PDF is a picture of your stamp. It doesn't certify the document, and it is no substitute for a notary or an official seal.

Step 1: make a clean stamp impression

The photo can only be as good as the impression on paper.

  • Use plain white paper. Lined or colored paper leaves marks that are hard to remove.
  • Re-ink the stamp and press it evenly, without rocking. Try a few impressions and keep the sharpest.
  • Let the ink dry for a minute so it doesn't smudge while you photograph it.
  • Leave space around it. Don't stamp near the edge of the sheet or near other writing.

Step 2: photograph it well

  • Take the photo in daylight near a window, without flash. Flash makes a bright spot in the middle.
  • Hold the phone straight above the paper, not at an angle, so a round stamp stays round.
  • Fill most of the frame with the stamp, but keep a little paper on every side.
  • Watch for your phone's shadow. If it falls on the paper, move so the light comes from the side.

Step 3: remove the background

A stamp image needs a transparent background: only the ink stays, and the paper disappears. This is what lets the stamp sit naturally over a signature line or printed text, the way a real stamp does.

You can do this in a photo editor that removes backgrounds, or in a signing app that cleans stamp photos for you. Check the result at full size. Good signs: the edges of the letters are smooth, the inside of the ring is clear, and there is no gray haze where the paper was. If the ink looks broken, go back to Step 1 with a fresh impression.

Keep the ink color of your real stamp. Blue, violet and red are all common, and a stamp that suddenly turns black looks odd to people who know your paperwork.

Step 4: place it on the PDF

  • Where: usually beside or slightly over the signature, in the space marked for the company or "for and on behalf of". Some businesses place it partly over the signature. Follow what the recipient asks for.
  • How big: about the size of your real stamp when the page is printed. Measure your stamp with a ruler and match it on an A4 or Letter page. A stamp that fills half the page looks wrong in print.
  • Don't cover amounts, dates or names. The reader must still be able to read the text under the stamp.

Step 5: stamp every page, if asked

Some agreements and tender documents ask for the company stamp on every page, the same way people initial every page of a PDF. Put it in the same corner on each page so the document looks consistent, and check the page count afterwards.

What the built-in phone tools can do

The pen tools in Google Drive, Files by Google and the iPhone's Markup are built for drawing and signatures. They don't offer a simple way to clean up a stamp photo and place it with a see-through background. For a stamp, you will need a photo editor plus a PDF app that can place images, or one app that does both. Our guides on signing a PDF on Android and iPhone cover what those tools do well.

Common mistakes

  • A white square around the stamp. The background wasn't removed. Redo Step 3.
  • A gray or patchy stamp. The photo was taken in dim light or at an angle. Photograph it again in daylight.
  • A stamp on the wrong page. Scroll through before saving; contracts often have the signature block on the last page only.
  • Stamping a draft. Stamp only the final version, and keep a copy of what you sent.

Using a logo instead of a stamp

Some businesses don't own a rubber stamp and use their logo instead. A color logo usually shouldn't have its background stripped the same way, because the cleaning keeps only the dark parts. Use the logo file as it is, sized small, next to the signature.

Questions

How do I add a stamp to a PDF?

Make an image of your stamp with a transparent background, then place it on the PDF with an app that can add images, beside your signature. Save it as a new copy.

How do I make a digital copy of my company stamp?

Stamp once on plain white paper, photograph it from straight above in daylight, and remove the paper background so only the ink is left. Keep that image for every document.

How do I add a stamp to all pages of a PDF?

Place the stamp on the first page, then repeat it in the same spot on each page. Some signing apps can copy a mark to every page in one step.

Is a stamped PDF the same as a certified document?

No. A stamp image shows the document comes from your business, but it doesn't certify anything. If a document needs certification or notarization, ask the person who requested it what they accept.
