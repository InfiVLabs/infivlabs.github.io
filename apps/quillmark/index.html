Quillmark lets you sign a PDF on your phone in a few taps: open the file, place your signature, initials or the date, and share the signed copy. There is no account to create and nothing is uploaded. Your documents, signatures and stamps stay on the phone, and the app works offline.

What you can do with it

  • Sign a PDF three ways. Draw your signature with a finger, type your name in one of 10 handwriting styles, or photograph your signature on paper and let Quillmark lift the ink off the page.
  • Add the small marks paperwork asks for. Initials, today's date, your printed name, a tick or a cross. Drag and resize each one until it sits on the line.
  • Initial every page in one step. Place your initials once and copy them to the same spot on every page, or on every page except the last.
  • Add your company stamp. Stamp once on white paper, take a photo, and the stamp becomes an image you can place on any PDF.
  • Sign paper documents too. Scan pages with the camera, or turn photos from your gallery into a PDF, then sign it.
  • Let someone else sign on your phone. The "Other signer" option lets a second person sign, and their signature is not kept.
  • Keep your original. Your marks go into a new signed copy. You can share it by email or any app, save it to the device, or print it.

Who it is for

Anyone who gets a PDF and needs to send it back signed today: a lease, an offer letter, a school permission slip, a delivery note or a client contract. Freelancers and small businesses use the stamp for their own paperwork.

Signing for free, and what Premium adds

Your first 3 signed documents are free with no watermark, and you see the count before you start. Saving a document again that already counted is free. Premium removes the limit and adds initials on every page and the company stamp. Plans are weekly with a 3-day trial, yearly, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

What kind of signature this is

Quillmark adds a simple electronic signature: an image of your signature, initials or stamp placed on the PDF. It is not a certificate-based digital signature, such as a qualified signature or one tied to a government ID. Many everyday agreements are signed this way, but some documents need a wet signature or a certificate. The guides below explain the difference.

This is general information, not legal advice. If you are not sure what a document needs, ask the person who sent it or a lawyer.

Questions

Can I sign a PDF without making an account?

Yes. Quillmark has no account and no server. You open the PDF from Files, Mail, WhatsApp or Drive, sign it on the phone, and the signed copy is saved on the phone.

Is it free to sign a PDF with Quillmark?

Your first 3 signed documents are free, with no watermark on them. After that you can watch an ad to sign one more document, or choose Premium for unlimited documents.

Can I sign a PDF offline?

Yes. Opening, signing and saving all happen on the device, so you can sign with no connection and send the file once you are back online.

Does Quillmark fill in form fields?

No. Quillmark places signatures, initials, dates, names, ticks and stamps on the page. For PDF forms with fields to type into, use a form-filling app.
