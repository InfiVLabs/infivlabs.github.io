How to sign a PDF on Android

How to sign a PDF on Android: step by step with Google Drive, Files by Google or Samsung Notes, plus what a signing app adds and how to send it back.

Updated 26 Nov 20265 min read

To sign a PDF on Android without installing anything, open it in the Google Drive app, tap the pen (Annotate) button at the bottom right, sign with your finger, and save a copy. That gives you a hand-drawn signature on the page. If you need a saved signature you can reuse, typed initials, the date or a stamp, use a PDF signing app instead.

Sign a PDF with Google Drive

Google Drive comes installed on most Android phones, and its PDF viewer has a drawing tool.

  1. Save the PDF to Drive, or open it from Drive if it is already there. From Gmail, you can tap the Drive icon on the attachment to add it.
  2. Open the PDF in the Drive app.
  3. Tap the Annotate button (the pen) in the bottom-right corner.
  4. Pick the pen, then a thin size and a dark color such as black or blue.
  5. Zoom in on the signature line and sign with your finger or a stylus. Use undo if the first try looks wrong.
  6. Save. Drive lets you save over the original or save a copy under a new name. Save a copy, so the unsigned version stays clean.

Drive's pen is freehand only. It does not keep your signature for next time, and it does not type text, so the date and your printed name have to be handwritten too.

Sign a PDF with Files by Google

  1. Open the PDF in Files by Google.
  2. Tap Edit at the bottom right.
  3. Choose the pen and sign on the page.
  4. Tap Save copy at the top right, then Upload.

The signed copy goes to your Google Drive, and the original stays on the phone unmarked. If you need the signed file on the phone, download it from Drive afterwards.

Sign a PDF on a Samsung phone

Galaxy phones include Samsung Notes, which can import a PDF and let you write on it.

  1. Open Samsung Notes and choose Import PDF (from the create button or the plus menu).
  2. Pick the PDF. Notes makes a copy, so the original file is untouched.
  3. Sign with your finger or the S Pen.
  4. Open More options and choose Save as file, then PDF.

Menu names change a little between One UI versions, so look for the same words if your screen differs.

When a signing app is worth it

The built-in tools work for a single signature. They get slow when a document asks for more: initials on every page, the date in three places, your printed name under the signature, or a company stamp. A signing app saves your signature once, places it where you tap, and writes everything into a new signed copy.

Before you trust one with a contract, check three things: whether it needs an account, whether it uploads your document to a server, and whether the free version adds a watermark. Adobe retired its separate Fill & Sign app and now points people to Acrobat Reader for filling and signing.

Sending the signed PDF back

  • By email: open the signed copy, tap Share, choose your mail app, and reply to the sender with it attached.
  • On WhatsApp or another chat app: share the file as a document so it arrives as a PDF, not as a photo.
  • Check before you send: open the signed copy once. Make sure the signature sits on the line, every page is there, and you are sending the signed file, not the original.

Common problems

  • The PDF won't open for editing. It may be password-protected. Ask the sender for an unlocked copy.
  • The signature looks thick or shaky. Zoom in further before drawing, or sign larger and shrink it afterwards if your tool allows resizing.
  • You signed the wrong copy. If you saved over the original, ask the sender to send it again. Saving a copy every time avoids this.
  • The form has boxes to type into. Drive's pen can't type into form fields. Use an app that fills forms, then sign.

What kind of signature this is

A signature drawn or placed on a PDF is a simple electronic signature: an image on the page. In the US, the ESIGN Act says a signature can't be refused legal effect only because it is electronic, and in the EU the eIDAS rules say the same for simple electronic signatures. Neither makes an image signature the same as a certificate-based digital signature, and some documents, such as wills, are outside these rules.

This is general information, not legal advice. If a document says it needs a digital certificate, a notary or a wet signature, follow that, and ask a lawyer if you are unsure.

Questions

How do I sign a PDF on my Android phone for free?

Open the PDF in the Google Drive app, tap the pen button at the bottom right, sign with your finger and save a copy. Files by Google and, on Galaxy phones, Samsung Notes can do the same.

Can I sign a PDF on Android without an app?

You need some app to open the PDF, but Google Drive and Files by Google are already installed on most Android phones, so you don't have to download anything new.

How do I sign a document and send it back from my phone?

Sign a copy of the PDF, open it once to check it, then tap Share and send it by email or as a document in your chat app.

Why can't I type my name on the PDF in Google Drive?

Drive's annotate tool only draws. To type your name or the date, write it by hand with the pen, or use an app that can add text.
