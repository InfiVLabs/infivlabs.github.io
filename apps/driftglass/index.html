Driftglass: a private sobriety counter for every sober day

Sober day tracker with a daily pledge, urge help and milestones. Fully private.

Driftglass is a sobriety counter app that shows exactly how long it has been since your last drink, down to the minute. Each counter is drawn as a piece of sea glass that gets smoother as your days sober add up, from a sharp fresh break on day one to fully tumbled at one year.

It works for alcohol and for other habits people count days away from: drugs, gambling, porn, sugar or late-night scrolling.

What the sober day tracker does

  • Counts your clean time. Days, hours and minutes since your start, plus the money kept and the hours regained if you enter what the habit used to cost you.
  • Lets you choose how a day counts. Either one day per full 24 hours since your start, or one day per midnight passed.
  • Marks milestones. 24, 48 and 72 hours, 1 and 2 weeks, 30, 60 and 90 days, every month to a year, 18 months, 1,000 days and then every year. Each one drops a new piece of glass into your jar.
  • Starts the day with a pledge. Hold the glass for a moment to promise one sober day. In the evening, a short check-in asks how it went, how strong the urges were and what was behind them.
  • Keeps your past days. A reset never erases anything. Earlier runs, total clean days and your longest stretch stay on record.

Help for the hard minutes

Long-press the glass or open Help when an urge hits: breathe for two minutes, play the tape forward, read your reasons, ride the wave with a ten-minute timer, or call someone from your own list.

Support lines for the US, UK, Ireland, Canada, Australia and New Zealand, plus an international directory, are behind the lifebuoy on every screen.

Private by design

There is no account and no feed, and your records stay on your phone. Lock the app with a PIN or biometrics, keep reminders discreet and export everything as JSON or CSV.

Free and Plus

The counter, the urge tools, the support lines, the lock and export are free. Driftglass Plus adds unlimited counters, the full journal with search, full patterns such as top triggers and risky hours, and extra share-card styles. Plus comes as a yearly plan with a free trial, a monthly plan or a one-time purchase.

Questions

How do I count my days sober?

Count from your sobriety date, the first day you didn't drink. On that date you have zero full days; a day later you have one. Our guide on how many days sober you are walks through the counting rules with worked dates.

Can I track more than one habit?

The free plan counts one habit. Plus counts alcohol, sugar, scrolling or anything else side by side, each on its own piece of glass.

What happens to my count if I drink again?

You reset the counter, and your earlier days stay saved. Driftglass shows your total clean days and your longest stretch, so the time you already put in still counts.

Does Driftglass help with Dry January?

Yes. In the two weeks before January it offers to join a Dry January pack, then tracks each pledged day on a 31-dot strip. See our Dry January 2027 guide for a plan for the month.

Driftglass is a self-tracking tool, not a treatment program or a medical device. If you drink heavily every day, stopping suddenly can be dangerous: talk to a doctor before you stop. In the US, the SAMHSA National Helpline is free and open 24/7 at 1-800-662-4357. In the UK, call Drinkline on 0300 123 1110.
