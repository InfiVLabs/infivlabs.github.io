Sewsie: a doll coloring and dress up game for kids

Color glitter dresses, sew them onto your doll, dress up and play offline

Sewsie is a coloring and dress-up game for children aged about 4 to 10. Your child colors a garment, taps "Sew it on", and watches a running stitch fix it onto the doll. Then she dresses the doll, picks a scene and keeps the look in a lookbook. It works without internet and needs no account.

What your child does in Sewsie

  • Colors a garment. Tap a part of a dress, coat or skirt to fill it with a plain color, a soft blend, glitter, or a print: dots, stripes, checks, hearts, stars or flowers. Undo is one tap away.
  • Sews it on. The finished piece flies to the doll and a stitch line runs around it as it lands.
  • Dresses the doll. Racks hold dresses, tops, skirts, coats, shoes, bags, hats and necklaces to mix and match. Tap the doll and she twirls, with the skirt and hair swinging like real fabric.
  • Makes the doll her own. Eight skin tones, thirteen hair colors and nineteen hairstyles, plus touches such as freckles, glasses, vitiligo and hearing aids, so children can make a doll that looks like them.
  • Chooses a scene. The atelier, a garden party or the runway, then a photo for the lookbook.

Every outfit and look saves by itself, and a new design is free to color each day.

What grown-ups should know

  • Offline. All coloring and dressing works with no connection, which makes it a good fit for car rides and flights.
  • No account, no sign-up. Drawings and looks are stored on the device. The privacy policy lists exactly what is and is not collected.
  • A grown-ups' question. Purchases, sharing a picture, settings and links out of the app sit behind a simple multiplication question.
  • What is free. Twenty garments, three scenes and the daily design. One payment, with no subscription, opens every garment and scene, including the Winter Ball, and the seasonal packs that follow.
  • Ads on Google Play. The free Android version shows a few child-safe, non-personalized ads. A child can choose to watch a short video to open one garment for good, and a full-screen ad can follow a finished creation, never when the app opens and never while coloring or dressing. The one-time unlock removes all ads.

How Sewsie differs from other coloring games

In most coloring apps the game ends when the picture is filled. In Sewsie the dress your child colored is the one the doll wears, twirls in and poses in for a photo. The doll is an original character, and the app looks like a tailor's workroom, with pattern paper, pins and fabric swatches.

Questions

What age is Sewsie for?

Children aged about 4 to 10. Younger ones can tap-fill and dress the doll without reading; older ones spend longer on blends, prints and matching outfits.

Does Sewsie work offline?

Yes. Coloring, dressing, scenes and the lookbook all work without a connection. Ads in the Google Play version only load when the device is online.

Are there ads in Sewsie?

On Google Play, the free version shows child-safe, non-personalized ads, and buying the unlock removes them. Ads never appear when the app opens or while your child is coloring or dressing the doll.

Can my child buy things by accident?

Purchases sit behind a grown-ups' question, and there is only one item to buy: a one-time unlock with no subscription.
