Slipway – Arrow Escape Puzzle: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Slipway is a puzzle game that runs entirely on your device.

What we collect

  • Nothing that identifies you. The app has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own.
  • Journey level, prints, Daily results, coins, settings and purchases are stored only on your device. You can export them as a file from Settings, and you can delete them by uninstalling the app.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob shows ads to players who have not bought Remove Ads or the Slipway Pass. AdMob may use the advertising identifier to serve and measure ads. In the EEA and UK you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before any ad loads, and you can change your choice in Settings. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • RevenueCat processes purchase receipts so your purchases can be restored on a new device. It receives an anonymous app user id and the store receipt, never your name or email. Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play handle payment.

Crash reports and usage statistics

Google Firebase Analytics records how the app is used (events such as "board cleared", with an app-instance identifier and device information) and Firebase Crashlytics sends crash logs and diagnostics, so we can fix bugs and improve the app. This data is never used for advertising, and no name, email or account is included. Google's policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Sharing

When you share a Daily card or a board code, the share sheet sends only the image and text you see, to the app you pick.

Children

The app is rated for ages 13 and up and does not knowingly collect data from children.
