Runstarter: learn to run 5K with a walk-run plan

Learn to run 5K: a walk-run plan that adapts to you, with a voice coach

Runstarter is a running app for beginners who want to learn to run. Its nine-week walk-run plan starts with one minute of running at a time, with walking in between, and builds to 30 minutes of running without stopping, which is about 5K for many people. A voice coach tells you when to run and when to walk, over your own music.

How the plan works

There are three runs a week, 27 in all. Each one starts and ends with a five-minute walk. Week 1 alternates one minute of running with a minute and a half of walking; by week 6 the walking breaks are gone, and week 9 is a steady half hour. You can see every session in the 9-week beginner running plan.

Not ready to run yet? A six-week walking plan builds a base first, from short brisk stretches to 30 minutes of brisk walking, and then one tap moves you to the running plan. See starting to run when you're out of shape.

A plan that bends

  • After each run, say how it felt: you could chat, you could just about talk, or you couldn't talk. After a hard one the plan offers to repeat it, and after two hard ones in a row, to step back one run.
  • Missed a few sessions? The app suggests where to pick up again.
  • Prefer two runs a week? The same runs, in the same order, over about 14 weeks.
  • Got a race? Set its date and the plan tells you when to start to finish in time.

Made for a phone in a pocket

  • The coach speaks over your music: music dips for a moment and comes back, and podcasts pause and resume.
  • Timer, voice and GPS keep going with the screen locked.
  • On a treadmill the same sessions run on time alone, with no location needed.
  • Run with a friend: one phone shows a code, the other scans it, and both hear every change at the same second.

Every finished run gets an ink stamp on your plan card, and you can share a finished stage as an image.

Private, and free to start

There is no account. Runs and routes stay on your phone, and shared cards show stats, never a map. Weeks 1 and 2 of the running plan and the first week of the walking plan are free, with a small banner on the home screen and never an ad during a run. The rest is a one-time purchase or a yearly subscription with a free trial; either removes ads.

New to exercise, or living with a heart or joint condition? Check with a doctor before you start. Stop and walk if you feel pain, dizziness or chest discomfort.

Questions

Can I really go from no running to 30 minutes in nine weeks?

Many beginners do, because each week asks only a little more than the last. If a week feels too hard, repeat it; taking longer is fine.

Is 30 minutes of running the same as 5K?

Only at about 6 minutes per kilometer (9:39 per mile). Many beginners cover 4 to 5 km in that time, which is normal. See average 5K times for paces.

Does it work on a treadmill?

Yes. A treadmill run follows the same session on time alone, without GPS, and you can type in the distance afterwards.
