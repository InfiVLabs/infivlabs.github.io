Average 5K time by age, gender and for beginners

The average 5K time for US race finishers in 2018 was 39:02 (men 35:22, women 41:21). Times by age group, beginner times and a pace table.

Updated 26 Nov 20264 min read

The average 5K time in US races is about 39 minutes. An analysis of 2.6 million US 5K results from 2018 by RunRepeat found an average finish of 39:02: 35:22 for men and 41:21 for women. Race fields include people who walk part or all of the course, so a beginner who runs a 5K in 35 to 45 minutes is right in the middle of the pack.

Three things to keep in mind when you read this table:

  • These are race finishers, not all runners. People who enter a race are more active than average. On the other hand, charity and fun runs include walkers, which pulls the averages up.
  • Averages hide a wide spread. In every age group, some people finish far faster than the average and others take far longer.
  • Times differ by course and weather. A hilly course or a hot day can add minutes.

How long does it take a beginner to run 5K?

A beginner's easy running pace is often somewhere between 6:30 and 9:00 per kilometer. At those paces 5K takes about 33 to 45 minutes. Here is how pace translates into a finish time.

What is a good 5K time for a beginner?

For a first 5K, a good time is one you finish while running at a pace you could keep talking at. That might be 40 minutes, it might be 32. Compared with the averages above, anything under about 40 minutes for women or 35 minutes for men is at or ahead of the typical US race finisher.

Running 5K in 30 minutes needs a pace of 6:00 per km, or about 9:39 per mile. How long it takes to get there varies a lot from person to person, and a slower first 5K is just as much of a finish.

How to get faster at 5K

Don't try to get faster in your first two months. Run all your runs at an easy, talking pace and build to 30 minutes first. After that:

  1. Run three times a week, consistently. Regular running improves times more than any single hard workout.
  2. Make one run a little longer, 35 to 45 minutes at an easy pace.
  3. Add a few short pick-ups to one run a week: 20 seconds at a faster pace, then walk or jog until your breathing settles, repeated four to six times.
  4. Run a 5K every month or two to see progress. Your second 5K is often faster than your first, because you know how to pace it.

Common mistakes

  • Comparing yourself with runners who have trained for years. Compare with your own last run.
  • Starting the race too fast. The first kilometer feels easy with adrenaline. Start slower than your goal pace.
  • Judging a run by pace alone. Heat, hills, wind and sleep all change it.

Questions

What is the average 5K time?

About 39 minutes for US race finishers: 35:22 for men and 41:21 for women, according to RunRepeat's analysis of 2018 results.

Is a 30-minute 5K good?

Yes. It is faster than the average finish for every age group of both men and women in RunRepeat's US data.

What is a good 5K time for a beginner?

Anything you can finish while running at a comfortable pace. For many beginners that is between 33 and 45 minutes.

How long does it take to walk 5K?

About an hour at 3 miles per hour, or about 47 minutes at a fast 4 miles per hour.
