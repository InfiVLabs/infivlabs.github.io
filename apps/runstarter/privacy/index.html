Runstarter privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026 (camera for "Join a friend" added). Runstarter is published by InfiV Labs ("we"). Contact: infivlabs@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your plan progress, run history, how each run felt (if you answer), run days, reminder times, race date and settings are stored only on your device. We have no server and no account system, and we never receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it. An export file is created only when you choose "Export runs", and it goes wherever you send it; routes are left out of it unless you switch them on.

Location

Runstarter asks for your location only when you start an outdoor run, and uses it only while that run is going, to measure distance and pace. It works while the app is open or showing its run notification; it never asks for location in the background and never tracks you between runs. A simplified route of each run is kept on your phone. Routes and locations are never uploaded, never sent to us or any third party, and never used for ads or analytics. Treadmill runs use no location at all. Shared card images show your stats, never a map.

What third-party services receive

  • Google AdMob (only for free-tier users, and only after consent where the law requires it): device advertising identifier, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions. Used to show and measure ads. Ads never appear during a run. Google privacy policy.
  • Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics: app usage events (for example "run completed", with the week and day of the plan, never distance, pace or location), device model, OS version and crash reports. Used to understand use and fix crashes. Ad personalisation signals are switched off.
  • RevenueCat and Google Play Billing: purchase and subscription status, and an anonymous app user ID. Used to unlock the full plan and restore purchases. RevenueCat privacy policy.
  • Your phone's speech engine: the coach's lines are spoken by the voice built into your phone, on the device.
  • Google Play services barcode scanning: when you choose "Join a friend", camera frames are read on your phone to decode your friend's QR code. No image is stored or sent to us, and the code only holds which run to start and when.

Permissions

Location (while using the app): outdoor runs only. Camera: only on "Join a friend", to read a friend's code. Notifications: the run notification with its Pause button, and run-day reminders if you switch them on. Foreground service: keeps the timer, coach and GPS running with the screen locked, only while a run is going. Vibration: a buzz at each change between running and walking.

Your choices

You can run without GPS, reopen the ad consent form in Settings → Privacy options (where available), turn reminders off, reset your advertising ID in your device settings, and delete all app data by uninstalling. Runstarter is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. It is a fitness plan, not medical advice; check with a doctor before you start if you are new to exercise or have a heart or joint condition.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when the app's data use changes.
