Unskein: a calm untangle rope game with no timer

Relaxing tangle rope puzzle and brain teaser: untie knots offline, no timers

In review at Google Play

Unskein is a rope untangle puzzle game. Wool ropes are tied between wooden pegs on a linen board, and they cross each other. You lift a rope end, tie it to an empty peg, and keep going until no rope crosses another. There is no clock, no lives and no energy bar, so a knot can take one minute or twenty.

How the rope puzzle works

  • Every crossing carries a red pin, and the counter at the top says how many are left.
  • Drag a rope end to a free peg, or tap the end and then tap the peg.
  • Hold an end over a peg and hollow pins show what that move would cross before you let go. Drop it anywhere else and the end springs back.
  • When the last pin comes out, the ropes untwist and the knot is done.

The board is seen straight from above, so no rope hides behind another and no crossing is ever out of view.

What is in the game

  • 10,000 levels along a yarn thread, ten to a skein. The first 40 are set by hand as a gentle ramp; the rest are built on the phone.
  • Raised pegs from level 20. A rope tied between two raised pegs floats over the ropes on the linen and never crosses them, which changes how a tangle is solved.
  • A Daily knot, the same for everyone. It is small on Monday and grows to a big one on Sunday, with a streak calendar and an optional reminder.
  • Knots for friends. Make a fresh tangle in one of three sizes, solve it, and send it. Anyone can type its six-letter code to play the same knot.
  • A Yarn Album. Each finished skein is knitted into a swatch, from seed stitch to fair isle.

Every board starts untangled and is then tangled with real moves, so every knot has a way back. Hints follow that way.

Who it is for

Unskein suits people who want a slow logic puzzle for a lunch break, a commute or the half hour before bed, and untangle players who are tired of countdowns and lost lives. Comfort settings include patterned ropes for color-blind players, calmer motion, tools on the left for left-handed players and a warm fireside mode for evenings.

Questions

Is there a timer in Unskein?

No. There are no timers, no lives and no move limit. Moves are counted only for stars: three stars means you matched par without a hint or a spare peg.

Can you play Unskein offline?

Yes. Levels are generated on the phone, there is no account, and your progress stays on your device. You can play a full session in airplane mode.

Is Unskein free?

It is free to play. An ad can show between levels, never in the first ten and never in the middle of a knot. A one-time purchase removes every ad and gives five free hints a day instead of two. Hint packs are optional.

What happens if I get stuck on a knot?

Undo takes back your last three moves at no cost. A hint shows the next move toward the answer, and if you have wandered off that path, a free rewind takes you back to the last board that was still on it.
