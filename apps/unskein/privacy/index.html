Unskein – Rope Untangle Puzzle: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Unskein is a rope untangle puzzle game that runs entirely on your phone.

What we collect

  • Nothing that identifies you. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of our own.
  • Your levels, stars, Daily knot results, hints, album and settings are kept only on your phone. Android may

include them in your phone's own backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps; you can turn that off

in Android's backup settings. Uninstalling the app deletes them from the phone.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob shows ads to players who have not bought Remove Ads. AdMob may use the advertising identifier to

serve and measure ads. In the EEA and UK you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before any ad

loads, and you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Google's policy:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

  • RevenueCat processes purchase receipts so Remove Ads can be restored on a new phone. It receives an anonymous

app user id and the store receipt, never your name or email. Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

  • Google Play (and the App Store on iPhone) handle payment.

Crash reports and usage statistics

Google Firebase Analytics records how the app is used (events such as "level complete", with an app-instance

identifier and device information) and Firebase Crashlytics sends crash logs and diagnostics, so we can fix bugs and

tune the levels. In the EEA and UK neither starts until you agree, in Google's consent form, to information being

stored on your device, and both stop if you change that choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices. This data is never

used for advertising, and no name, email or account is included. Google's policy:

https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Sharing

When you share a Daily knot or a knot code, the share sheet sends only the card and text you see, to the app you

pick: the knot as it started, your stars and moves, and a Google Play link. The link carries the knot's code or the

Daily knot's date, so a friend who installs Unskein from it finds that knot waiting. A knot code describes the knot,

nothing about you.

Links you installed from

On its first launch the app asks Google Play once which link it was installed from (the Play Install Referrer). If

that link names a shared knot, the app keeps the knot's code on your phone until you play it. The referrer is read

on your phone and never sent anywhere.

Daily knot reminder

If you switch on the Daily knot reminder, the app schedules notifications on your phone at the time you choose.

They are local notifications: nothing is sent to us or to a server, and you can switch them off in Settings or in

Android's notification settings.

Children

The app is meant for ages 13 and up and does not knowingly collect data from children.
