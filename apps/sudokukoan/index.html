Calm daily sudoku. Hints teach the technique, 12 lessons, large print, offline.

Sudoku Koan is a sudoku app for phones and tablets that teaches you how to solve, one technique at a time. Ask for a hint and it tells you which row, column or box is doing the work and names the technique, so you learn the move instead of copying a digit. Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be finished by logic alone, with no guessing.

What you can do with it

  • Learn sudoku techniques in order. Twelve lessons, each built on a real board position, then short drills: hidden single, naked single, pointing pair, box line reduction, naked and hidden pairs, triples and quads, X-Wing and Y-Wing. A mastery map shows which ones you can already spot.
  • Get hints that explain. A hint first points you to the right area, then explains the logic and names the technique. It only fills the cell if you ask.
  • Play a daily sudoku. One fresh board each day, gentle early in the week and hardest on Saturday, with a calendar, a yearbook of finished puzzles and a streak that forgives one missed day a week.
  • Check a puzzle from the newspaper. Type in the 81 cells and the analyzer tells you whether it has exactly one solution, grades it by the techniques it needs, and lets you play it with the same hints.
  • Choose how strict the game is. Zen mode only marks a digit that repeats in its row, column or box. Classic flashes a wrong digit and keeps it out. Challenge gives you three lives.

Who it is for

It suits players who can finish an easy sudoku but stall on hard ones. If you are starting from zero, the guide on how to solve sudoku step by step covers the rules and the first two techniques.

It is also built for tired eyes. Large type mode gives bigger digits, thicker lines and 72dp number keys. There is a dark theme, highlight colors that color blind players can tell apart, reduced motion, and screen reader labels on every cell. More on that in large print sudoku for seniors.

How the difficulty levels work

There are four levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert. A puzzle's level is set first by the hardest technique it needs, then by how many digits it starts with. Easy and Medium need only singles. Hard adds pointing pairs and box line reduction. Expert adds pairs, triples, quads, X-Wing and Y-Wing. The sudoku techniques guide works through each of these with example grids.

Questions

Is Sudoku Koan free?

Yes, all four difficulties are free to play, along with the first three lessons, three explained hints a day and the last seven daily puzzles. One purchase unlocks all twelve lessons, unlimited explained hints and the full daily archive, and removes the ads.

Does it show ads while I play?

Ads never appear on the board itself. The free version shows a banner on the home and results screens and an occasional full-screen ad between puzzles, never in your first three puzzles. The one-time purchase removes them.

Does Sudoku Koan work offline?

Yes. Every puzzle, lesson and hint works offline, with no account and no sign-up. Your progress stays on your phone, and you can export a backup from settings.

Will I ever have to guess?

No. Every puzzle in the app has exactly one solution and can be solved with the twelve techniques it teaches. For any puzzle, see what to do when you are stuck at sudoku.
