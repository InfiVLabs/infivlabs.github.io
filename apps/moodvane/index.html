Moodvane: a mood tracker app that shows what lifts your days

Log your mood in two taps and see which habits go with your better days

Moodvane is a mood tracker and mood diary app. You log how you feel in two taps on a barometer dial, mark what you did that day, and after a week it starts showing which activities go with your better days.

It is for people who want to understand their moods without writing pages every night: students in exam season, people with busy jobs, anyone who has started to wonder why some weeks feel heavier than others.

How mood tracking works in Moodvane

  1. Swing the needle. Five readings run from Stormy through Rain, Change and Fair to Very dry. A brass hand stays on your last reading, so you see the change: "Fair, up from Rain".
  2. Tap what you did. Activities such as a walk, friends, a deadline or good sleep come in seven groups, and you can add your own.
  3. Add a line if you like. A short note, or something you are grateful for with a prompt to start you off. Or skip it.

You can log several times a day, from the app, the evening reminder or, on Android, a home-screen widget. Night owls can set the day to end at 1, 2, 3 or 4 am.

What lifts you: patterns, not guesses

Moodvane compares the days with an activity against the days without it, and only shows a pattern when both sides have enough days. Each card says how much stands behind it, for example: on days with Walk, mood was higher, +0.8 (18 days with, 42 without, strong pattern). These are patterns, not causes, and the app says so.

You also get a month calendar in weather colors, a trend line, the mix of readings and the weekdays that read highest and lowest. A month can be shared as a climate card that shows colors only, never notes.

Private by design

There is no account. The journal stays on your phone. A PIN or fingerprint lock, CSV and JSON export and a password-encrypted backup are free. If you are coming from another mood app, you can import its CSV or JSON export.

On a hard day

After a Stormy reading or a few low ones in a row, Moodvane offers a minute of slow breathing and the crisis lines for your country.

Free and Pro

Logging, activities, notes, gratitude prompts, the month calendar, the top pattern, the lock, export and backup are free. Moodvane Pro adds every pattern (including pairs of activities and the day-after effect), the Year view, a one-page monthly PDF you can take to a therapist, more reminders and unlimited activities. Pro comes as a lifetime option, or yearly with a 7-day free trial, or monthly.

Questions

How do I track my mood?

Pick a simple scale, log at the same time each day, and note two or three things you did. After a few weeks, compare your better days with the rest. Our guide on how to track your mood goes step by step.

Is there a printable mood calendar?

Yes. Our 2027 mood calendar is a year in pixels grid you can print and fill in by hand, with a color key.

Can I write gratitude notes too?

Yes. Each entry has an optional gratitude line, with a new prompt each day from a set of 120. You can also browse our gratitude journal prompts.

Is Moodvane a mental health app?

No. It is a self-reflection tool, not a medical or mental-health service, and it does not diagnose or treat anything.

If you are struggling, you don't have to wait for a pattern. In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), free and open 24/7. In Canada, call or text 9-8-8. In the UK and Ireland, call Samaritans free on 116 123. In Australia, call Lifeline on 13 11 14. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number.
