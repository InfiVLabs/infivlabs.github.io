Mood calendar 2027: a printable year in pixels you fill in by hand

A printable 2027 mood calendar: a year in pixels grid with 12 months by 31 days, a five-color key, and how to read your year when it's full.

Updated 26 Nov 20265 min read

A year in pixels is a mood calendar with one small square for every day of the year. Each evening you color that day's square for your overall mood, using a short color key. By December, the 365 squares of 2027 show your year at a glance: the heavy weeks, the bright stretches and the patterns you would never see day to day.

The grid below has 12 columns (January to December) and 31 rows (the day of the month). Squares marked x don't exist in 2027: there is no February 29, 30 or 31 (2027 is not a leap year), and April, June, September and November have 30 days.

How to use this year in pixels template

  1. Print the page (landscape fits best), or copy the grid into a notebook. Drawn by hand, a grid of 12 columns by 31 squares fits on one sheet of US Letter or A4.
  2. Pick your five colors from the key below and color the key itself first, so you never have to guess.
  3. Fill in one square each evening, for the day as a whole, not the last hour. If you miss a day, fill it the next morning or leave it blank: a blank square is honest data too.
  4. Write a word in the margin on unusual days, such as "exam", "flu" or "vacation". Those notes are what make the pattern readable later.

Color key: a five-point mood scale

ScoreMoodSuggested colorBlack-and-white mark
5Great: energetic, happy, at easeYellowFill the square solid
4Good: a pleasant, steady dayGreenCross (X)
3Okay: neutral, mixed or flatLight blueSingle diagonal line
2Low: tired, irritable, sad or worriedGrayDot in the center
1Very low: a hard dayDark blue or purpleSmall circle

Five levels are enough to see a pattern without agonizing over each square. If you want more detail, keep the color for overall mood and add a tiny mark for one other thing, such as a dot for a bad night's sleep.

How to read your year in pixels

When a few months are filled in, look for:

  • Clusters, such as three gray days every month, or a run of yellow in summer.
  • Weekdays, such as Sundays that are often lower than Fridays.
  • Before-and-after, such as how the week after a big deadline looks compared with the week before.
  • Seasons, such as whether the darker months look different from the lighter ones.

Treat what you see as a question, not a verdict. A gray cluster before every exam tells you something; it doesn't tell you why. Our guide on how to track your mood covers how to add a little context so the patterns mean more.

Common mistakes with a mood calendar

  • Scoring the moment you fill it in instead of the whole day. A bad final hour can paint a good day gray.
  • Using too many colors. Ten shades look lovely and are hard to read in December.
  • Skipping hard days. The low squares are the ones you'll most want to understand later.
  • Changing the key mid-year. If you must, start the change on the 1st of a month and note it.

Questions

What is a year in pixels?

It is a mood calendar with one square for each day of the year, colored for that day's overall mood. Filled in daily, it shows a whole year of moods on one page.

How many squares are in the 2027 year in pixels?

There are 365. The grid has 372 positions (12 by 31), and the seven that don't exist in 2027 are marked x: February 29, 30 and 31, plus the 31st of April, June, September and November.

What colors should I use for a mood tracker?

Any five you can tell apart at a glance. One easy choice runs from dark blue for very low days to yellow for great days. Color the key first and keep it the same all year.

Can I start a mood calendar in the middle of the year?

Yes. Start today and leave the earlier squares blank. A few months of honest squares are more useful than a perfect grid you never begin.

Doing this in Moodvane

Moodvane keeps the same kind of record on your phone. Each reading on its five-step barometer dial colors the day in the month calendar, and the Year view in Pro lays out the whole year like the grid above: one column per month, one square per day, each in its reading's color. Because you also tap what you did each day, the app can compare days with and without each activity and show which ones go with your better days.

A mood calendar is a self-reflection tool, not a diagnosis. If you notice a long run of low days, or you feel unsafe, talk to a doctor or someone you trust. In the US, call or text 988, free and open 24/7. In the UK and Ireland, call Samaritans free on 116 123. In Australia, call Lifeline on 13 11 14.
