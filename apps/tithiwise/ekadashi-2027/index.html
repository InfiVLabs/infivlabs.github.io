Ekadashi 2027 list: every date, for India and the USA

Ekadashi 2027 list with all 25 dates for India (New Delhi) and the New York dates where they differ, plus Vaishnava days and how parana works.

Updated 26 Nov 20265 min read

There are 25 Ekadashis in 2027, starting with Saphala Ekadashi on Sunday 3 January and ending with another Saphala Ekadashi on Thursday 23 December (India dates). The list below gives each date as calculated for New Delhi, the Vaishnava (ISKCON) day where it differs, and the New York date for the 11 Ekadashis that fall a day earlier in the eastern US.

Dates depend on where you live, because an Ekadashi is fixed by local sunrise. Smarta and Vaishnava calendars sometimes differ by a day. This page is a guide, not a substitute for your temple's calendar or your family priest.

The month names follow the purnimanta reckoning used in North India. In the amanta reckoning used in Gujarat and Maharashtra, a Krishna paksha Ekadashi belongs to the previous month: Saphala Ekadashi in January is in Margashirsha there, not Pausha. The Ekadashi and its date are the same either way.

Two published panchangs agree on every India date above. One other calendar lists Nirjala Ekadashi as 14 or 15 June; if your family keeps Nirjala, check which day your temple observes.

For New York, the dates in the last column come from calculations for New York City. Toronto, New Jersey and the rest of the US East Coast usually match New York, but a city farther west can differ again, so check the date for your own city.

Why Ekadashi is on a different day in the USA

A tithi is a stretch of the Moon's movement, so it begins and ends at the same instant all over the world. What changes from place to place is the clock time of that instant and the time of sunrise. The general rule is that the vrat is kept on the day whose sunrise falls inside the Ekadashi tithi.

Take Saphala Ekadashi in January 2027. Its tithi runs from 2:24 PM on 2 January to 4:08 PM on 3 January, Indian time.

  • In New Delhi, sunrise on 2 January comes hours before the tithi begins, while sunrise on 3 January falls inside it. So India keeps Sunday 3 January.
  • In New York, which is 10 hours 30 minutes behind India in winter, the same tithi runs from 3:54 AM on 2 January to 5:38 AM on 3 January. Sunrise on 2 January is inside it, and by sunrise on 3 January the tithi is already over. So New York keeps Saturday 2 January.

That is why US calendars printed with India's dates can be a day off for about 11 Ekadashis in 2027. The same thing happens with festivals; see the Hindu festivals 2027 list.

Smarta and Vaishnava Ekadashi

Most households follow the Smarta date, which is the first date in each row. Vaishnava (ISKCON) calendars apply stricter rules about the dawn period before sunrise, so when the Ekadashi tithi spreads over two sunrises they may fast on the second day. In 2027 the India calendars give a separate Vaishnava day three times: Pausha Putrada (19 January), Kamika (30 July) and Rama (26 October). Abroad the split days can be different ones, so ask your temple which day it observes.

How the fast is broken (parana)

An Ekadashi fast is broken on the next day, Dwadashi, after sunrise. Panchangs give a parana window for each Ekadashi, worked out from two rules:

  1. Wait until Hari Vasara is over. Hari Vasara is the first quarter of the Dwadashi tithi.
  2. Break the fast before Dwadashi ends, unless Dwadashi has already ended before sunrise.

Because both depend on local sunrise and on when Dwadashi begins, the parana window can fall in the early morning one month and in the afternoon another. Read it for your own city rather than copying a time from India.

Common mistakes

  • Using an India calendar abroad. On the US East Coast this puts 11 of the 25 Ekadashis of 2027 on the wrong day.
  • Reading the tithi start as the fast day. The tithi often begins the afternoon before. The fast follows sunrise, not the moment the tithi starts.
  • Mixing up Smarta and Vaishnava days. If your family follows an ISKCON temple, use its calendar for the three split days.

Questions

How many Ekadashis are there in 2027?

  1. A calendar year holds 24 or 25 Ekadashis depending on where the dates fall. In 2027 Saphala Ekadashi comes at both ends of the year, on 3 January and 23 December (India dates).

When is the next Ekadashi after Diwali 2027?

Prabodhini (Devutthana) Ekadashi on Wednesday 10 November 2027 in India, and Tuesday 9 November in New York.

Why is Ekadashi on a different day in the USA?

Because the vrat follows local sunrise. The Ekadashi tithi ends at the same instant worldwide, but US clocks run 9 and a half to 13 and a half hours behind India's, so the sunrise that falls inside the tithi is often the previous day's.

Which Ekadashi is Nirjala Ekadashi in 2027?

Nirjala Ekadashi is on Monday 14 June 2027 in India. It is the Shukla paksha Ekadashi of Jyeshtha, kept without water by many families.
