Makar Sankranti 2027: date, punya kaal and why some calendars say 14 January

Makar Sankranti 2027 is on Friday 15 January in India: the Sun enters Makara at 9:14 PM on the 14th. Punya kaal times and the US date.

Updated 26 Nov 20265 min read

Makar Sankranti 2027 is observed on Friday 15 January in India. The Sun enters Makara (sidereal Capricorn) at 9:14 PM Indian time on Thursday 14 January, after sunset, so the punya kaal for the holy dip, puja and daan moves to the morning of the 15th. In New Delhi the punya kaal runs from 7:15 AM to 5:46 PM on 15 January, and the maha punya kaal from 7:15 AM to 9:00 AM.

Times here are for New Delhi unless a city is named. Your own punya kaal follows your city's sunrise and sunset. For the time of a ritual, follow your temple or family priest.

Why some calendars say 14 January

You will see both 14 and 15 January for Makar Sankranti 2027, and both have a reason:

  • The astronomical moment is on the 14th. The Sun's crossing into Makara happens at 9:14 PM on 14 January, Indian time. Some lists, including the Government of India's 2027 restricted holiday list, give 14 January.
  • The observance is on the 15th. The traditional rule is that when the Sankranti moment comes after sunset, the punya kaal and its rituals are moved to the next sunrise. Sunset in New Delhi on 14 January is well before 9:14 PM, so panchangs set for Indian cities keep 15 January.

If your family follows a printed calendar or a local temple, use its day. The difference is a question of which rule a list applies, not an error.

Makar Sankranti 2027 abroad

The Sankranti moment is one instant worldwide, so what decides the day abroad is whether that instant falls in your daytime.

  • US East Coast. 9:14 PM in India is 10:44 AM in New York on 14 January. That is daytime, so the punya kaal starts at 10:44 AM and Makar Sankranti is kept on Thursday 14 January, a day before India.
  • UK. The moment is 3:44 PM in London on 14 January, before sunset there, so by the same rule the UK day is also 14 January. Check the exact punya kaal for your own city.
  • Australia. In Sydney the moment comes at 2:44 AM on 15 January, in the night, so the observance there moves to the morning of 15 January.

Families abroad often follow an India calendar for Sankranti and end up a day late. The same pattern affects Ekadashi: see why Ekadashi falls on a different day in the USA.

What punya kaal means

Punya kaal is the stretch of the day counted as most meritorious for the Sankranti rituals: the bath, often in a river, the Surya puja and giving daan such as til, jaggery, rice, khichdi, blankets or clothes. The maha punya kaal is a shorter, more preferred part of it near the start.

Panchangs measure the punya kaal from the Sankranti moment (or from sunrise, when the moment was the night before) for a set span, cut off at sunset. Different panchangs use slightly different spans, so the end time you see can vary by a few minutes or more between sources.

One festival, many names

Makar Sankranti is a solar festival. It follows the Sun's entry into Makara rather than a tithi, which is why it stays on 14 or 15 January every year while lunar festivals such as Holi or Diwali move by weeks. Across India it goes by several names:

  • Makar Sankranti in most of North and West India, with til-gud sweets and a holy dip.
  • Uttarayan in Gujarat, known for kite flying.
  • Pongal in Tamil Nadu, a harvest festival that begins on 15 January 2027.
  • Magh Bihu in Assam.
  • Lohri in Punjab, celebrated with a bonfire on the evening before Sankranti. Calendars list Lohri 2027 on 13 or 14 January, so check locally.
  • Khichdi in parts of Uttar Pradesh and Bihar, after the dish that is cooked and given away.

Planning the day

  1. Find your city's punya kaal. If you are outside India, check whether the Sankranti moment falls in your daytime on the 14th.
  2. Plan the bath and puja for the maha punya kaal if you can, since it is the preferred window.
  3. Keep the daan ready the night before: til, jaggery, rice and dal for khichdi, or warm clothes, as your family's custom is.
  4. For kite flying in Gujarat, the public holiday may not match the punya kaal date. The festival day and the office holiday are separate questions.

Questions

Is Makar Sankranti on 14 or 15 January 2027?

In India it is observed on Friday 15 January 2027. The Sun enters Makara at 9:14 PM on 14 January, after sunset, so the rituals move to the next morning. Some holiday lists still give 14 January.

What time is Makar Sankranti punya kaal in 2027?

In New Delhi, 7:15 AM to 5:46 PM on 15 January, with the maha punya kaal from 7:15 AM to 9:00 AM. Other cities differ with their sunrise and sunset.

When is Makar Sankranti 2027 in the USA?

Thursday 14 January 2027. The Sankranti moment is 10:44 AM in New York, in daytime, so the punya kaal starts then.

Why is Makar Sankranti always in January?

Because it is fixed by the Sun's entry into Makara, not by a lunar day. That crossing happens on 14 or 15 January each year.
