Tithiwise privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026. Tithiwise is published by InfiV Labs ("we"). Contact: infivlabs@gmail.com.

This page is published at https://infivlabs.github.io/apps/tithiwise/privacy.

What stays on your phone

Your chosen language, saved cities, month-naming and sunrise settings, and which festivals and vrats you want

reminders for are stored only on your device. The panchang, festival dates and reminders are calculated on the

phone from bundled data. We have no server and no account system, and we never receive these settings.

Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Tithiwise does not ask for your location. You pick a city from a bundled list; that choice is not sent to us.

What third-party services receive

  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (only when you do not have Tithiwise Plus, and only

after consent where the law requires it): device advertising identifier, IP-derived approximate location,

device and app information, and ad interactions. Used to show, measure and limit ads, and to record your consent

choice. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and

[how Google uses data from partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

  • Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics: app usage events (for example "festival opened" or "reminder turned

on", with your app language and the country of your chosen city, never the city itself), an app instance

identifier, device model, OS version and crash reports.

Used to understand which features are used and to fix crashes. See the

[Firebase privacy page](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

  • RevenueCat and Google Play Billing: purchase and subscription status and an anonymous app user ID. Used to

activate Tithiwise Plus and restore purchases. Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see your card

details. See [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

We do not sell your data, and we do not combine it with other sources to identify you.

Permissions

  • Notifications: requested only when you turn on reminders, to send a note the evening before a festival or

vrat. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): so scheduled reminders survive a phone restart.

Your choices

  • Reopen the ad consent form in Settings → Privacy options (where it applies).
  • Remove ads entirely with Tithiwise Plus.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Delete all app data by uninstalling the app. To ask about data held by the services above, write to

infivlabs@gmail.com and we will help you contact them.

Children

Tithiwise is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data

from children.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when the app's data use changes.
