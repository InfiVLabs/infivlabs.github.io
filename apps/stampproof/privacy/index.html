StampProof – Timestamp Camera: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

StampProof is a camera for work photos. It stamps the time, place and a proof code on each photo and keeps your

jobs and reports on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Photos, jobs, notes, reports and settings are stored in the app's own storage on your phone. There is no

account, no sign-in and no server of ours that receives them. If you switch on "Also save to gallery" for

a job, stamped copies are also added to your phone's gallery. Android may include the app's data in your phone's

own backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps; you can turn that off in Android's backup settings.

Uninstalling the app deletes its data from the phone.

  • Camera. Used only to take the photos you shoot. Nothing is recorded in the background, and there is no

microphone access.

  • Location. Used, while the camera is open, to print the coordinates and accuracy on the photo. It is saved

only with the photos you take. If you refuse location, photos are stamped "No location".

  • Address lookup. To print a street address, the app passes the coordinates to your phone's built-in address

lookup (Android's Geocoder, which on most phones is provided by Google). We never receive them.

  • Proof codes are calculated on the phone from the picture and its facts, with a random value made on your

phone at install. Verify compares a photo or code with the records on the same phone.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob shows ads to users who have not bought StampProof Pro: a banner on the Jobs list, and in some

countries an occasional full-screen ad after a report is shared. AdMob may use the advertising identifier to

serve and measure ads. In the EEA and UK you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before any ad

loads, and you can change your choice in Settings → Privacy options. Google's policy:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

  • RevenueCat processes purchase receipts so StampProof Pro can be restored on a new phone. It receives an

anonymous app user id and the store receipt, never your name, email, photos or locations. Policy:

https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

  • Google Play (and the App Store on iPhone) handle payment.

Crash reports and usage statistics

Google Firebase Analytics records how the app is used (events such as "photo captured" or "report exported", with

an app-instance identifier and device information) and Firebase Crashlytics sends crash logs, so we can fix bugs.

Events never include your photos, notes, addresses or coordinates. In the EEA and UK neither starts until you agree

in Google's consent form, and both stop if you change that choice in Settings → Privacy options. Google's policy:

https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Sharing

When you share a photo, a report or a share card, the share sheet sends only that file and its text to the app you

pick (for example WhatsApp or your email app). The text includes a Google Play link. Report PDFs contain the photos,

times, addresses, coordinates and codes you chose to include.

Children

The app is meant for work use by adults and does not knowingly collect data from children.
