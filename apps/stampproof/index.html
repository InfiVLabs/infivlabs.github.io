StampProof: a timestamp camera and GPS camera for job photos

Stamp time, GPS and address on job photos, then send a PDF photo report.

StampProof is a timestamp camera app for work photos. Every shot carries the date and time to the second, the GPS position, the street address and your note, burned into the picture itself. Photos go into job folders, and a job becomes a PDF photo report you can send before you leave the site.

What goes on the stamp

The card you see on the camera is the one burned into the photo:

  • Date and time to the second, with the UTC offset, so the time zone is clear.
  • The street address and the GPS coordinates, with their accuracy in meters or feet.
  • The job name, your note and a short proof code.

Pick the corner, or a band along the bottom, and one of eight styles: Field, Minimal, Big text for chat apps, Delivery, Inspector, Attendance, Survey and Night. The date, time and position also go into the photo's metadata, so it is a geotagged photo as well as a stamped one.

With no signal, the stamp still shows the time and the coordinates; the street address needs the phone's own address lookup.

Jobs and before/after pairs

Every photo lands in a job, laid out like a job sheet. In pair mode the earlier shot shows as a faint guide on the camera, so the after photo matches the angle of the before. A pair can be shared as one before/after card.

Photo reports

Report turns a job into a PDF: a cover with the newest shot, then one, two or four photos per page or side-by-side before/after spreads, each with its time, place and code, and a log at the end. Tap the labels on the page to show or hide notes, location and codes. Send it to WhatsApp, an email app or Downloads. If you are building one by hand, the photo report template shows what to include.

Verify, and what it can show

Each photo gets a short code made from the image and its stamp facts. Verify checks a file or a typed code against the record on the phone that took it, and tells you whether the photo and its facts have changed since capture in StampProof. It cannot tell whether the phone's clock or GPS were right. Photos are marked "Unverified time" or "Unverified location" when the phone reported a simulated location or a clock that disagreed with GPS time. More on this in how to tell when and where a photo was taken.

Who it is for

Roofers and contractors sending before and after photos to a client or adjuster. Couriers keeping proof of delivery. Landlords and tenants recording move-in condition. Field reps logging store visits with a geotagged photo at each stop.

Questions

Is StampProof free?

It is free to start. The free plan stamps unlimited photos with the Field, Minimal and Big text styles, keeps three active jobs, and makes reports of up to 12 photos, with a small StampProof mark. Pro adds your company name and logo, all eight styles, unlimited jobs and pairs, longer reports and CSV export.

Can StampProof add a date to photos I already took?

No. A stamp is made at the moment of capture, from the clock and GPS at that moment. Stamping an old photo would put a time on it that the app never saw.

Do I need an account?

No. Photos, jobs and reports stay on your phone, in the app's own folder. You can turn on "also save to gallery" for a job.
