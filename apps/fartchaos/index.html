Silly party boat racing powered by toots! Play offline or with friends on Wi-Fi.

Fart Chaos is a silly party racing game for Android where the only engine is gas. Four critters ride one round raft down wild rivers: hold TOOT to blast across the water, slide along your edge to steer, and grab power-ups to stink up your rivals. Race bots offline, or play local multiplayer with friends on the same Wi-Fi, up to 8 players. No account, no sign-in.

How it plays

Quick, funny party races

A show is three races: Normal, Hard, then Advanced. The last boat in each race is knocked out, and whoever wins the final is the CHAMPION. Every show builds fresh rivers, so no two races are the same.

Play with friends on the same Wi-Fi

QUICK MATCH puts phones on the same Wi-Fi in the same race. LOCAL TEAM opens a room friends join with a four-letter code. In CREW mode friends share one raft (DUO, TRIO or SQUAD) and steer it together; toot at the same moment for a SYNC boost.

Daily Race and the Long River

The Daily Race is a new course every day, the same for everyone: beat your best time and keep your streak. The Long River is one giant river in nine parts, from easy waters out to the sea, with every checkpoint saved.

Seven silly power-ups

Bean Blast, Cabbage, Mint Shield, Stink Bomb, Whoopee Mine, Chili Cannon and the Stink Storm that zaps whoever is in first.

Make your critter

Six critters with their own voices and toots, twelve colors and ten hats to start, and more looks to earn by playing. Nothing you buy changes how you race.

Questions

What is Fart Chaos?
Fart Chaos is a silly party boat racing game for Android by InfiV Labs. Four critters ride one round raft down wild rivers and the only engine is gas: hold TOOT to move, slide along your edge to steer, and use power-ups on your rivals.
Does Fart Chaos need the internet?
No. You can race bots offline. Playing with friends needs the same Wi-Fi network or a phone hotspot, not the internet.
How many people can play together?
Up to 8 players on the same Wi-Fi. Bots fill any empty seats, so a race always starts.
Can friends ride the same raft?
Yes. In CREW mode two, three or four friends share one raft and steer it together, and tooting at the same moment gives a SYNC boost.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no account and no sign-in.
Is Fart Chaos free? Is it pay to win?
It is free to play. At most one ad shows after a finished show, never during a race. Purchases only add looks or remove ads, so nothing you buy changes how you race.
When is Fart Chaos available?
Fart Chaos is in closed testing on Google Play for Android and is coming soon to everyone.
