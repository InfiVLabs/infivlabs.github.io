Quadrille: a habit tracker drawn on squared paper

Habit tracker on a weekly spread: a fair streak, widgets and seasonal packs.

Quadrille is a habit tracker for Android that looks like a page in a squared notebook. Each habit is a row, each day is a square, and you fill today's square with one tap. The streak follows the schedule you set, so a planned rest day never breaks the chain. There is no account: your habits stay on your phone.

What you can track

Write one line for each thing you want to keep doing and give it a drawn mark or any emoji. A habit can be a simple yes or no ("Make the bed") or a count ("8 glasses of water", "10 pages"), which fills with tally marks as you go. Each habit has its own schedule:

  • every day
  • set days of the week
  • a number of times a week, such as three workouts
  • every other day

If you need ideas, the editor suggests common rows (read, walk, water, journal, study, sleep by 23:00), and our list of 100 habits to track has more.

A streak that stays fair

A streak counted against the calendar makes a "three times a week" habit look broken on its four days off. Quadrille counts against your own schedule instead. You can also mark a planned rest day (two a month for free, four with Pro); it sits hatched on the paper without breaking anything. Every seventh filled square earns a freeze that covers one missed day, because research on habit formation found that missing a single day does not undo the work. More on that in how long it takes to form a habit.

Your year on one page

Each habit gets a year page: twelve months of squares, filled days per month, and the weekday that slips most. You can share this week or the whole grid as a picture of the notebook page.

Seasonal spreads

Packs add a whole page of habits in one go. There is a New Year page of six small habits for January, a Ramadan page from suhoor to taraweeh, and an exam-season page with study blocks, past papers and sleep. The New Year habit tracker guide explains how to set one up for 2027.

Reminders and the home screen

A reminder arrives only on days a habit is due, with a Done button on the notification. A home-screen widget shows today's rows so you can check in without opening the app. There is a dark "night edition" of the paper for evenings.

What is free

Three habits stay free for good, with the widget, every year page, packs, reminders and export. Quadrille Pro adds habits without limit, a note on any day, ruled or dotted paper, four rest days a month, no ads and share cards without the footer, as a monthly or yearly plan or a one-time purchase.

Questions

Does Quadrille need an account?

No. Your habits and squares are stored on your phone. You can export them as CSV, or everything as JSON, at any time, and delete everything with one tap.

Can I track a habit that is not daily?

Yes. Set it for chosen weekdays, a number of times a week, or every other day. The streak only counts the days the habit is due.

Is there a printable version?

The app is digital, but if you like paper too, our printable habit tracker template has a weekly grid and a spreadsheet version you can copy.
