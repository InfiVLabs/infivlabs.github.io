Habits to track: 100 ideas for your habit tracker

100 habits to track, sorted by area, each with a clear rule for what counts as done. Plus how many habits to track at once and daily versus weekly.

Updated 26 Nov 20264 min read

Good habits to track are small, specific and visible: you can tell at a glance whether today's square is filled. Below are 100 habit tracker ideas in ten areas, each written with a rule for what counts as done. Pick three to start, not thirty.

How many habits should you track?

Start with three, and stay below six until those feel easy. Every row you add is a square that can stay empty, and a page full of gaps makes people quit the whole tracker. Add a new habit when an old one no longer needs effort, which for many people takes about two months (see how long it takes to form a habit).

Daily or weekly?

Track a habit daily when it fits every day (water, a short walk, bedtime). Give it a weekly target when daily would be unrealistic (workouts, a long call, batch cooking). A "3 times a week" habit should not count four rest days as misses, so set the schedule to match.

How to turn an idea into a row you can tick

  • Give it a number or a time. "Drink more water" becomes "8 glasses"; "sleep earlier" becomes "in bed by 23:00".
  • Attach it to something that already happens. After coffee, after brushing teeth, on the train.
  • Write the minimum version. On a bad day, one page or one push-up still fills the square.
  • Phrase it as something you do. "Fruit with breakfast" is easier to keep than "no junk food".

Doing this in Quadrille

In Quadrille each idea above becomes a row on a squared-paper page. Counted habits such as 8 glasses or 10 pages fill with tally marks, and weekly targets like "3 times a week" only count the days they are due. The editor suggests common rows, and seasonal packs add ready-made pages for the New Year, Ramadan and exam season.

Questions

What are good habits to track?

Small actions you can do on most days and check at a glance: water, a short walk, reading 10 pages, a fixed bedtime, one line in a journal. Choose ones tied to a goal you care about.

How many habits should I track at once?

Three is a good start and six is a sensible maximum. Add another when one of the first ones feels automatic.

Should I track habits daily or weekly?

Daily for things that fit every day, weekly targets for things that need more time or recovery, such as workouts. Set the schedule so rest days do not count as misses.
