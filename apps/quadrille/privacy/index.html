Quadrille – Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026. Quadrille is made by InfiV Labs. Contact: infivlabs@gmail.com.

The short version

Quadrille has no account and no server of its own. Your habits, the squares you fill, your notes and your settings are stored only on your phone. We never see them. The app talks to three services, each for one job: Google Play and RevenueCat for purchases, Google AdMob for the one banner that free users in some regions see, and Firebase for anonymous usage statistics and crash reports.

What stays on your phone

  • Your rows (habit names, schedules, colours), every filled, rested or frozen square, and your notes: in a database in the app's private storage.
  • Your settings (week start, day boundary, theme, reminders).
  • The data the home-screen widget shows (today's rows) and check-ins made from the widget or a notification before the app next opens: in the app's private preferences.

Android may include this data in your device backup to your Google account if you have backups turned on. Exports you make (CSV, or everything as JSON) go only where you send them through the share sheet.

What leaves your phone, and why

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Google Play Billing and RevenueCatPurchase receipts and product ids, an anonymous app user id generated on the deviceTo unlock Quadrille Pro and restore it on a new phone
Google AdMob (free users whose device region is one where Quadrille shows ads; elsewhere the AdMob SDK never starts)The advertising id, approximate location from the IP address, ad impressions and clicks, device diagnostics; in the EEA and UK only after you answer the consent formTo show the banner on a year page
Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google)Anonymous app events (a square filled, a paywall seen, a pack added), the Firebase app-instance id, device model and OS version, crash logsTo see which features are used and to fix crashes

Analytics never receives habit names or notes: events carry only categories such as "custom" or "template". Analytics data is never used for advertising; the app sets Firebase's ad-related consent flags to denied.

These companies process the data as our service providers under their own policies: Google and RevenueCat.

Permissions

  • Notifications: asked only when you turn on a reminder. Reminders fire on the days a row is due; "Done" on the notification fills the square.
  • Run at start-up (Android): to put reminders back after a restart.
  • Quadrille does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, health data or files.

Reminders and the widget

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone; nothing about them is sent anywhere. The home-screen widget reads the app's own private data and writes your taps back to it.

Your choices

  • Export your data any time: Settings → Your data → Export. Delete everything: Settings → Your data → Delete everything, or uninstall the app.
  • Ads: Quadrille Pro removes all ads. Where the consent form was shown (EEA and UK), Settings → Privacy options reopens it. You can reset or delete your advertising id in your phone's Google settings.
  • Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Children

Quadrille is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.
