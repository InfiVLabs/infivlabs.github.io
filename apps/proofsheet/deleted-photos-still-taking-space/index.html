Deleted photos still taking up space on Android

Deleted photos still taking up space on Android? They sit in a Trash for 30 days. Where each Trash is, how to empty it, and what else keeps storage full.

Updated 26 Nov 20264 min read

If your Android phone's storage didn't go down after you deleted photos, the photos are most likely sitting in a Trash. On Android 11 and later, deleting usually moves a photo to a Trash where it keeps using storage for about 30 days, so it can be restored. To get the space back now, empty the Trash in the app you deleted from: Google Photos, your phone's gallery app or Files by Google.

Why deleted photos still use storage

The Trash is a safety net. Nothing is erased at once; the file is only marked as trashed and hidden. Android's own media library gives each trashed item an expiry date, typically 30 days later, and removes it after that. Until then the bytes stay on the phone.

Several apps keep their own Trash, and each one only shows what was deleted inside it. That is why "I emptied the Trash" and "my storage is still full" can both be true.

Google Photos used to keep backed-up photos in the Trash for 60 days. Google's help page now says 30 days for deleted photos and videos.

Empty the Trash in Google Photos

  1. Open Google Photos.
  2. Tap Collections at the bottom, then Trash.
  3. Tap More, then Empty Trash, then Delete permanently.

Emptying it deletes everything there for good. If you only want to remove a few items, tap Select, pick them, and choose Delete permanently.

One thing to know before you delete in Google Photos: for a backed-up photo, the Trash button removes it from your Google account and from every device with backup on, as well as from this phone. If you only want it off the phone, select it, tap More and choose Delete from device instead.

Empty the Trash on a Samsung phone

  1. Open Gallery, tap the Menu icon (three lines), then Trash.
  2. Touch and hold a picture, then tap All to select everything.
  3. Tap Delete all and confirm.

Samsung's My Files app and some other Samsung apps keep their own deleted items too. For duplicates on Galaxy phones, see how to delete duplicate photos on Samsung.

Empty the Trash in Files by Google

Open Files by Google, tap Menu at the top left, then Trash (Bin in some regions). Select the items, or select all, and delete them. Duplicate files you removed from the Clean tab end up here and stay 30 days. Files cleared from the Junk files card skip the Trash and are deleted at once.

Still full after emptying every Trash?

  • The number lags. Storage figures can take a few minutes to update. Close Settings, wait, and open Settings > Storage again.
  • The photos had copies elsewhere. The same picture may also be saved in a chat app's folder, a screenshots folder, or an editing app's export folder. Chat media is the usual one; see WhatsApp images taking up space.
  • Photos weren't the problem. Settings > Storage shows whether apps, videos or system files are the bigger share. Our phone storage full guide covers the rest.

Before you empty a Trash

  • Scroll through it once. The Trash is the last chance to rescue a photo deleted by mistake.
  • Check that photos you care about are backed up. After the Trash is emptied, a photo that only lived on this phone can't be restored.
  • If you're not in a hurry, you can simply wait. The phone empties the Trash by itself when the 30 days are up.

Questions

Why is my storage full after deleting photos?

The deleted photos are in a Trash, where they stay for about 30 days and keep using space. Empty the Trash in Google Photos, your gallery and Files by Google to free it now.

How long do deleted photos stay in the Trash on Android?

Usually 30 days. Android's media library sets a 30-day expiry by default, and Google Photos, Samsung's Gallery and Files by Google all keep deleted items for 30 days.

Does deleting photos from Google Photos free up phone storage?

Yes, once the Trash is emptied. Deleting in the Google Photos app on Android also deletes the copy on the phone. Use Delete from device if you want to keep the backed-up copy.

Can I get back a photo after emptying the Trash?

Not from the phone. If the photo was backed up somewhere else, such as a computer or a cloud service, restore it from there.
