Proofsheet privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026. Proofsheet is made by InfiV Labs. Contact: infivlabs@gmail.com.

Your photos and videos stay on your phone

Proofsheet reads the photos and videos on your phone, with your permission, to find copies, similar shots, screenshots, chat media, large videos and blurry photos. All of this happens on the device. No photo, video, thumbnail or file name is uploaded, and we never see your library.

To make the next scan faster, the app keeps small measurements of each photo (a 64-bit fingerprint, a sharpness score and a brightness value), the list of items you asked never to be suggested, and a history of your cleanups (date, shelf, count and size). They are stored only inside the app and are removed when you uninstall it.

Proofsheet never deletes anything by itself. Every removal goes through Android's own confirmation and moves the items to your phone's Trash, where Android keeps them for 30 days unless you empty it.

No account

There is no sign-up and no account. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts or location.

Services inside the app

  • Google AdMob shows ads to users of the free plan. It may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions to show and measure ads. In the EEA and the UK the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings, Privacy options.
  • Google Play Billing and RevenueCat handle purchases. They receive your purchase records and an anonymous app user ID so that Pro works and can be restored. We do not receive your payment details.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics receive anonymous usage events (for example "scan finished", with the number of items and megabytes found) and crash reports, so we can fix problems. They never receive your photos or file names. Analytics data is not used for ads.

Permissions

  • Photos and videos: to find what you can clear. On Android 14 and later you can share only some photos; the app then checks only those.
  • Internet: for ads, purchases, analytics and crash reports. Your photos are never sent.

Children

Proofsheet is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices

You can withdraw photo access in Android Settings, change ad consent in the app's Settings, and remove all app data by uninstalling. For any question or request about your data, email infivlabs@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
