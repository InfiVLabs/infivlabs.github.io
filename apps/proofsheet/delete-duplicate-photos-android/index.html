How to delete duplicate photos on Android

How to delete duplicate photos on Android with Files by Google or Google Photos, how to clear similar shots and bursts, and how to get the space back.

Updated 26 Nov 20264 min read

To delete duplicate photos on Android, open Files by Google, tap Menu and then Clean, and on the Duplicate files card tap Select files. Keep the copy marked Original, select the others, and move them to the Trash. That clears exact copies. Similar photos, such as bursts and five tries at the same group shot, are not duplicates to Android, so you sort those yourself or with a similar-photo cleaner.

Most people find far more similar photos than true duplicates. A single burst can produce 20 nearly identical frames, and only one of them is worth keeping.

Delete duplicate files with Files by Google

Files by Google is installed on many Android phones and free on Google Play.

  1. Open Files by Google.
  2. Tap Menu, then Clean.
  3. On the Duplicate files card, tap Select files. If the card isn't there, the app hasn't found any copies.
  4. Leave the file with the Original badge unselected. Select the copies.
  5. Tap Move to Trash at the bottom and confirm.

The copies go to the Trash rather than disappearing at once, which brings us to the step most people miss.

Get the space back: empty the Trash

On Android 11 and later, deleted photos usually sit in a Trash for about 30 days before the phone removes them for good. Until then they still take up storage. If you need the space today, empty the Trash in the app you deleted from: Files by Google, Google Photos or your phone's gallery. Our guide on deleted photos that still take up space explains every Trash on the phone.

Clear similar photos by hand

Without a cleaner app, the quickest way through similar shots is to go month by month:

  1. Open your gallery app and switch to the view that shows days or months.
  2. Find a cluster of near-identical pictures: a burst, a group photo, a sunset.
  3. Open the first and swipe through. Zoom in on faces and text to check sharpness and closed eyes.
  4. Pick one keeper. Favorite it so you don't delete it by mistake.
  5. Select the rest of the cluster and delete them.
  6. Move to the next cluster, and empty the Trash when you are done.

Two tips that save time: start with your largest month (often a holiday or a wedding), and do bursts first, because they give back the most space for the fewest decisions.

Delete duplicates in Google Photos

Duplicates in Google Photos are often slightly different files: an edited copy, a screenshot of a photo, or the same picture saved from a chat. Search for the subject, compare the copies side by side, and delete the ones you don't need.

Be careful with what you delete in Google Photos. If a photo is backed up, deleting it in the app removes it from your account and from every device with backup on. If you only want it off the phone, select it, tap More and choose Delete from device.

Common mistakes

  • Deleting the original. Always keep the copy marked Original, or the oldest one, before clearing the rest.
  • Trusting "Select all" without a look. Glance through the selection first. A group of similar photos can hide one you love.
  • Forgetting the Trash. The space comes back only when the Trash is emptied.
  • Deleting a photo that only lives on this phone. If it isn't backed up and you empty the Trash, it is gone.
  • Mixing up phone storage and Google account storage. Deleting a photo only from the phone doesn't free any of the 15 GB in your Google account.

If you have a Galaxy phone, Samsung's Gallery and My Files apps have their own duplicate finders; see how to delete duplicate photos on Samsung.

Questions

How do I delete duplicate photos on Android for free?

Open Files by Google, go to Clean, tap Select files on the Duplicate files card, keep the Original, and move the copies to the Trash. Then empty the Trash to free the space.

How do I delete similar photos on Android?

Android has no built-in tool that picks the best of a burst. Go through your gallery cluster by cluster, keep the sharpest shot, delete the rest, or use a similar-photo cleaner that groups them for you.

How do I delete multiple photos at once on Android?

In most gallery apps, touch and hold one photo to start selecting, then tap or drag across the others. Tap the delete or trash button once for all of them.

Does deleting duplicates in Files by Google delete them from Google Photos?

Files by Google works on the files stored on your phone. Backed-up copies in your Google Photos account stay there until you delete them in Google Photos.
