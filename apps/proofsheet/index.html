Proofsheet: delete duplicate and similar photos on Android

Find duplicate and similar photos, keep the sharpest shot, clear the rest.

Proofsheet finds duplicate and similar photos on your Android phone, circles the sharpest shot in every burst, and lets you clear the rest in one go. It also lists large videos, old screenshots, chat media and blurry pictures, each with its size, so you can see where your phone storage went. Everything is checked on the phone, with no account and no uploads.

What it finds

The first screen shows the total you could free, in the same units as Android's storage settings, split into shelves with the biggest first:

  • Exact copies: the same file saved more than once. The oldest copy is kept.
  • Similar shots: bursts and retakes of one moment. The best one is circled, the rest are crossed out.
  • Large videos: videos over 100 MB, biggest first. You can set the line at 50, 100 or 250 MB.
  • Screenshots and screen recordings.
  • Chat media: photos and videos saved by WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal and other messaging apps.
  • Blurry photos: shaky or out-of-focus shots.

Duplicate finders such as the one in Files by Google look for copies of the same file. Proofsheet also groups photos that look alike, which is where most of the space goes after a burst or five tries at the same group photo.

How a cleanup works

Each group of similar photos appears as a film strip. The keeper gets a red circle and says why it won: the sharpest, the most detail or the best light. Tap any frame to change your mind, or keep the best in every group with one tap. When you move items, Android asks you to confirm, then puts them in the phone's Trash for 30 days. Proofsheet's Trash page shows how much space is waiting there and when Android empties it, with buttons to empty it now or put everything back.

Favorites are never offered for deletion, and anything you add to the skip list is never suggested again. Copies in Google Photos or other cloud backups are not touched: Proofsheet frees space on the phone, not in your Google account.

Who it is for

Anyone whose Android phone keeps warning that storage is almost full, and who would rather review their photos than let an app delete them blind. It runs on Android 11 and later. Proofsheet works only with photos and videos; it does not clean apps or system files.

Free and Pro

Scanning and reviewing are free, with 15 deletes a day; a short ad adds 15 more, up to three times a day. The free version shows ads. Pro removes the daily limit and the ads, as a weekly plan with a 3-day trial, a yearly plan, or a one-time purchase.

Questions

Does Proofsheet delete photos automatically?

No. It marks what it suggests, you choose, and Android asks you to confirm. Items go to the phone's Trash for 30 days, so you can put them back.

Does it upload my photos?

No. Photos are checked on the phone. There is no account and nothing is uploaded.

Is Proofsheet free?

Scanning and reviewing are free, with 15 deletes a day and ads. Pro removes the limit and the ads.

Does it work on Samsung phones?

Yes. Proofsheet works on Galaxy phones and other Android phones running Android 11 or later. On a Galaxy phone it reads the same photos and videos you see in the Gallery app.
