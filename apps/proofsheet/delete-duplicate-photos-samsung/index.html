How to delete duplicate photos on Samsung

How to delete duplicate photos on a Samsung phone with Gallery's Clean out or My Files, how to handle similar shots, and how to empty the Gallery Trash.

Updated 26 Nov 20264 min read

To delete duplicate photos on a Samsung Galaxy phone, open the Gallery app, tap the Menu icon (three lines), tap Clean out, then Delete duplicate pictures, and confirm with Delete duplicates. Samsung says this removes the copies and keeps the originals. Then empty the Gallery's Trash, because deleted pictures stay there for 30 days and keep using storage until you do.

Menu names change a little between One UI versions. If you don't see Clean out or Duplicate files, your phone may have no duplicates to show; Samsung notes the option can be hidden when there is nothing to clear.

Method 1: Gallery's Clean out

  1. Open Gallery.
  2. Tap the Menu icon (three horizontal lines).
  3. Tap Clean out.
  4. Under Clean out, tap Delete duplicate pictures.
  5. Look through what it found, then tap Delete duplicates.

Before step 5, scroll through the list. Duplicate finders compare files, and a picture you edited, cropped or saved again from a chat can count as a separate file or as a copy depending on the change. A minute of checking is cheaper than restoring photos one by one later.

Method 2: My Files

  1. Open My Files (it may be inside a Samsung folder on your home screen).
  2. Tap Analyse storage (some versions say Analyze storage or Storage analysis).
  3. Tap Duplicate files.
  4. Select the copies you want to remove and tap Delete.

Samsung's own warning: if you select every file in a group, the original goes too. In each group, leave one file unselected.

Similar photos: what Clean out doesn't catch

A burst of 15 shots, three retakes of the same family photo, or ten pictures of the same sunset are not duplicates. They are different files, so neither tool above deletes them.

Newer Galaxy phones can group similar-looking pictures in the Gallery, which helps you find them. Then it is a judgment call:

  1. Open a group and swipe through it.
  2. Zoom in on faces and text. Check for closed eyes, motion blur and focus.
  3. Keep the best one (tap the heart to favorite it).
  4. Select the rest and delete them.

Start with the months that had trips or events. They usually hold the biggest clusters.

Empty the Gallery Trash to get the space back

Samsung's Gallery keeps deleted photos and videos in its Trash for 30 days. Until then they still count against your storage.

  1. In Gallery, tap Menu, then Trash.
  2. Touch and hold a picture, then tap All in the top-left corner to select everything.
  3. Tap Delete all and confirm.

Only do this once you are sure; items deleted from the Trash can't be restored. If you used another app to delete, it may have its own Trash. Our guide on deleted photos still taking up space lists them all.

Before you start: a backup and a cloud check

  • Back up first if the photos matter. A cloud backup or a copy on a computer means a mistake costs nothing.
  • Check your Gallery's cloud sync. If your Gallery syncs with a cloud service, deleting a picture on the phone may also delete the cloud copy. Look in the Gallery settings before a big cleanup.
  • See where your space goes. Settings > Device care > Storage (Battery and device care on some versions) shows how much images, videos and apps use, so you know whether photos are really the problem.

Questions

How do I delete duplicate photos on my Samsung phone?

Open Gallery, tap Menu, then Clean out, then Delete duplicate pictures, and tap Delete duplicates. Then empty the Gallery Trash to free the space.

Does Samsung Gallery have a duplicate photo finder?

Yes. It sits under Menu > Clean out > Delete duplicate pictures. If you don't see it, the Gallery may not have found any duplicates.

How do I free up storage on a Samsung phone?

Check Settings > Device care > Storage to see what uses space, empty the Gallery Trash, delete duplicate and similar photos, remove large videos you have backed up, and uninstall apps you no longer use.

Why is my Samsung storage still full after deleting photos?

The photos are probably in the Gallery Trash, where they stay for 30 days. Open Gallery > Menu > Trash and delete everything there to get the space back.
