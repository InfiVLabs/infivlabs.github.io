Codelamp: an OBD2 code reader app that explains the check engine light

Read and clear check engine codes in plain English with any ELM327 adapter.

Codelamp is an OBD2 scanner app for Android and iPhone. Plug a low-cost ELM327 adapter into your car's diagnostic port, turn the key, and the app reads the trouble codes behind the check engine light. Each code comes back in plain English with an answer to the question most people actually have: is it OK to drive?

What you can do with it

  • Read trouble codes. Stored, pending and permanent codes, each shown as a tag you turn over to read its Health card.
  • Health cards. What the code means, how serious it is (Info, Soon, Now or Stop), whether you can keep driving, the likely causes with the cheapest checks first, and whether the job is DIY, a shop job or a major repair. Nearly 400 common codes are written up in our own words. Brand-specific codes say so instead of guessing.
  • Clear codes, then watch. Clearing is included. Before it sends the command, the app warns that the emissions self-tests reset and gives you five seconds to undo. It then re-checks the next three times you connect and tells you if a code came back.
  • Inspection pre-check. The car's emissions self-tests shown as one answer, so you know before a smog check or emissions test whether the car is ready.
  • Live data. Engine speed, coolant temperature, battery voltage and road speed on large gauges. Pro adds fuel trims, airflow, load, timing and every other value the car reports.
  • Mechanic report. A PDF with the codes, the car's facts and the self-test status, so you walk into the shop knowing what was read.

Which adapters work

Any adapter built on the ELM327 command set. On iPhone that means a Bluetooth LE or Wi-Fi adapter. On Android, classic Bluetooth adapters work too. The app asks what your adapter says on the box and tells you before you connect whether your phone can use it. If yours will not connect, the ELM327 troubleshooting guide walks through every common cause. No adapter yet? A demo car lets you try the app first.

Who it is for

Drivers who just saw the light come on and want to know if they can get to work. DIY owners who want live fuel trims and readiness without a shop tool. Anyone who wants to know the code before the shop quotes the repair.

How it differs

Many code readers show the raw standard text, such as "Catalyst System Efficiency Below Threshold", and stop there. Codelamp puts the decision first: drive on, book it in, or stop safely. It is honest that clearing turns the light off without fixing the fault. Above walking speed the screen shows two large gauges and a Stop button, nothing else.

Questions

Is Codelamp free?

Reading codes, the Health cards, clearing codes, four live gauges and the inspection pre-check are free. Codelamp Pro adds every live value, freeze frames, unlimited mechanic reports, more than one car and full trip history. It is sold yearly with a 7-day trial, monthly, or as a one-time purchase.

Does Codelamp work on iPhone?

Yes, with a Bluetooth LE or Wi-Fi ELM327 adapter. Classic Bluetooth adapters, the kind you pair with a PIN such as 1234, cannot connect to an iPhone.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, and your car data stays on your phone.

Is it a replacement for a mechanic?

No. Codelamp gives information, not a professional diagnosis. If the check engine light flashes, read what a flashing check engine light means and stop as soon as it is safe.
