ELM327 not connecting: how to fix your OBD2 adapter

ELM327 not connecting to your phone or car? Find out if your OBD2 adapter is Bluetooth, BLE or Wi-Fi, which work on iPhone, and the fix for each error.

Updated 26 Nov 20265 min read

Most ELM327 connection problems come down to three things: the adapter's radio does not suit your phone, it is connected the wrong way for its type, or the car is not switched on. An iPhone can only use Bluetooth LE (BLE) and Wi-Fi adapters, never classic Bluetooth ones. A classic Bluetooth adapter has to be paired in Android's settings first. And every adapter needs the key at ON before the car will answer.

First: which kind of adapter do you have?

Look at the box, the listing you bought it from, or the label on the adapter.

Some adapters are "dual" and offer both classic Bluetooth and BLE. On an iPhone only the BLE side can be used.

Why classic Bluetooth adapters do not work on iPhone

Apple lets apps exchange arbitrary data over classic Bluetooth only with accessories made under its MFi program. Bluetooth LE devices are not covered by MFi, so any app can talk to a BLE adapter. An Apple engineer put it plainly on Apple's developer forums: BLE devices "are not governed by MFi", while transferring data over classic Bluetooth needs the program. That is why the cheapest classic adapters, which are fine on Android, never show up on an iPhone. If you have an iPhone and a classic adapter, you need a BLE or Wi-Fi adapter instead.

OBD2 not connecting to Bluetooth: a checklist

Work down this list in order; the quickest checks come first.

  1. Turn the key to ON. The adapter gets power from the port, but the car's computer only answers with the ignition on. The engine can stay off to read and clear codes; it needs to run for live data like RPM.
  2. Check the adapter's light. No light means no power. The port is under the dashboard on the driver's side, usually above the pedals or behind a small cover. Push the adapter in firmly; a loose fit is common.
  3. Connect the right way for its type. A BLE adapter that you paired in iPhone or Android settings can be held by the system and hidden from apps. Forget it in settings and connect from the app. A classic adapter on Android works the other way round: pair it in settings first with PIN 1234 or 0000.
  4. Close other OBD apps. An adapter talks to one app at a time. Another app connected in the background will block it.
  5. Unplug the adapter for 10 seconds. This resets it when it refuses the connection or answers with nonsense.
  6. Check your car is OBD-II. In the US, OBD-II is required on gasoline cars and light trucks from the 1996 model year. In the EU, petrol cars have it from 2001 and diesels from 2004. Older cars may have a port that looks different, or none.

ELM327 Wi-Fi adapter not connecting

  • Join the adapter's Wi-Fi network in your phone's settings before opening the app. It usually has no password.
  • While you are joined, the phone has no internet over Wi-Fi. That is normal. Some phones then switch back to a network with internet by themselves; if yours offers to "stay connected", say yes.
  • Most Wi-Fi adapters use the address 192.168.0.10 and port 35000. If yours came with different numbers, enter those.

ELM327 connects but not to the ECU

If the app reaches the adapter but the car does not answer, the problem is between the adapter and the car:

  • The ignition is off or in accessory mode. Turn it fully to ON.
  • The adapter is still searching for the car's protocol. The first connection can take 10 to 20 seconds; let it finish.
  • The adapter is a poor copy. Some very cheap clones skip commands or support only some protocols. If values freeze or the car never answers, a better-made adapter is the fix.

ELM327 v1.5 vs v2.1

The ELM327 was a chip made by Elm Electronics, which announced in 2020 that it would close in 2022. Many adapters sold today run copied firmware, and the version number they report is not reliable: Elm Electronics never made a v1.5, and copies often report themselves as the newest version whatever code they actually run. Treat "v1.5" or "v2.1" on a listing as a label, not a guarantee. What matters more is the radio type in the table above and whether the seller says the adapter works with your phone.

One more thing: battery drain

Many adapters draw a little power all the time. If the car will sit for a few days, unplug the adapter so the battery stays healthy.

Questions

Why is my ELM327 not showing up on my iPhone?

It is almost certainly a classic Bluetooth adapter. iPhones only connect to Bluetooth LE and Wi-Fi OBD2 adapters. If the box mentions a PIN such as 1234 and not "LE" or "4.0", it cannot work with an iPhone.

Should I pair my OBD2 adapter in Bluetooth settings?

Only a classic Bluetooth adapter on Android. A Bluetooth LE adapter connects from inside the app, and pairing it in settings can stop apps from finding it.

Is a Wi-Fi or Bluetooth OBD2 adapter better?

Bluetooth LE is the easiest on both iPhone and Android because your phone keeps its internet connection. Wi-Fi adapters work on both too, but your phone loses internet over Wi-Fi while connected. Classic Bluetooth is fine on Android only.

What PIN does an ELM327 adapter use?

Classic Bluetooth adapters usually use 1234 or 0000. Bluetooth LE and Wi-Fi adapters normally have no PIN.
