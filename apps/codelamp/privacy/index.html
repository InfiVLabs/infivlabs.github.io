Codelamp privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026. Codelamp is published by InfiV Labs ("we"). Contact: infivlabs@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Codelamp has no accounts and no server. Everything it reads from your car through your OBD2 adapter (trouble codes, readiness self-tests, freeze frames, live values, the VIN, battery voltage and trips) is decoded and stored only on your device, and we never receive it. Your saved adapters and settings stay on the device too. Deleting the app deletes all of it. A backup file or a mechanic report is created only when you choose to make one, and it goes wherever you send it.

What third-party services receive

  • RevenueCat and the App Store or Google Play Billing, if you buy Codelamp Pro: the purchase receipt and an anonymous app user ID, used to unlock Pro and restore it on a new phone. They never receive your car data. RevenueCat privacy policy.
  • Google AdMob, only in the free version in India, Brazil, Mexico and Indonesia: a banner in the Garage and, only if you choose it, a short video that unlocks one more mechanic report. Ads never show while an adapter is connected. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, an approximate location derived from your IP address and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires consent you are asked first. How Google uses this data.
  • Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics: app usage events such as "scan complete" or "codes cleared" (counts and code families, never your VIN, your codes together with your VIN, or your location), an app-instance ID, device model, OS version, approximate location from the IP address and crash reports. Used to see which features are used and to fix crashes. Ad storage and ad personalisation are switched off for this data, so it is never used for ads. Google privacy policy.

Permissions

Bluetooth: to find and talk to your OBD2 adapter, asked when you first scan for one. Codelamp never uses Bluetooth to work out where you are. On Android 11 and older the system calls this "location" access, which it requires for any Bluetooth scan; Codelamp does not read your location. Local network (iPhone): to reach Wi-Fi OBD2 adapters on the adapter's own network.

Your choices

Change your ad consent any time in Settings → Privacy options; Codelamp Pro removes the ads. Reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Settings → Back up gives you all your data as a file. To have your purchase record deleted from RevenueCat, email us. Codelamp is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

When the app's data use changes, this page and its date are updated first.
