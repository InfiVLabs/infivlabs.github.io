Cellwise – Nonogram: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Cellwise is a puzzle game by InfiV Labs that runs entirely on your device.

What we collect

  • Nothing that identifies you. The app has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own.
  • Puzzle progress, settings, coins and purchases are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. Android may include them in your device backup. You can export them as a file from Settings and delete them by uninstalling the app.
  • The share card and the backup file go only where you send them from the share sheet.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob shows ads to players without Premium. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK may use the advertising identifier, device and app information, ad interactions and an approximate location derived from your IP address. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before any ad loads, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • RevenueCat processes purchase and subscription receipts so Premium can be restored on a new device and a lapsed subscription is recognised. It receives an anonymous app user id and the store receipt, never your name or email. Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Google Play (and the Apple App Store, where the app is offered there) handles payment and your subscription. We never see your card details.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google) tell us which features are used and why the app crashed. Analytics receives app interactions (for example that a puzzle was opened or solved), app and device information and a random app-instance id; Crashlytics receives crash logs and diagnostics (device model, OS version, app version, memory). None of it is linked to an account, and the app sets Google's consent flags so this data is never used for ads. Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Your choices

  • Change or withdraw ad consent: Settings → Privacy options.
  • Reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalised ads: your device's Google settings → Ads.
  • Export your data: Settings → Export progress. Delete it: uninstall the app.

Children

The app is rated for ages 13 and up and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the version on this page applies, with the date above.
