Cellwise is a nonogram app for Android and iPhone. The numbers beside each row and column tell you how many squares are filled, in order; fill the right ones and a picture appears. Every grid in the app has exactly one solution that you can reach by reasoning line by line, so you never have to guess.

What a nonogram is

A nonogram is a grid logic puzzle, also sold as griddlers, hanjie, picture cross or the Japanese crossword. A row clue of "3 1" means a run of three filled squares, at least one empty square, then a single filled square. You combine what the rows and the columns allow until every square is either filled or crossed out. No maths and no word knowledge are needed. If you are new to them, start with how to solve nonograms, with a worked 5×5 example.

What you can play

  • 1,144 puzzles up to 20×20, in black and white and in full color.
  • Color nonograms: each clue has its own color, and runs of different colors may touch without a gap.
  • A daily puzzle with a calendar and a streak: 10×10 from Monday to Thursday, 15×15 on Friday and Saturday, 20×20 on Sunday.
  • Endless mode, which builds new abstract mosaics on your phone and checks each one with the same solver.
  • A gallery of every picture you have revealed, with share cards and a time-lapse of your solve.
  • An Advent calendar in December: 24 winter puzzles, one door a day.

Why no puzzle needs a guess

Many nonograms, in apps and on paper, reach a point where the clues allow two different pictures. Then the only way on is to try a square and hope. Before a puzzle goes into Cellwise, a line solver works through it using the same row and column logic a person uses. If it cannot finish the grid, or if a second solution exists, the puzzle is rejected. Endless mosaics go through the same check on your device. The full reasoning is in nonograms without guessing.

Built for a phone screen

You drag to fill a whole run, with a live counter showing its length. Pinch to zoom a 20×20 grid while the clue bars stay pinned to the edge. Finished clues cross themselves off. A hint never fills a random square: it shows one the clues already force, so you can see the step you missed. Assisted mode flags a wrong square straight away; Relaxed mode, part of Premium, stays quiet like paper.

Who it is for

People who like Sudoku and want a puzzle that ends in a picture. Commuters who play without a signal: the app works offline, with no account. Players who have quit other apps over unfair late grids and want to finish every puzzle by thinking it through.

Questions

Is Cellwise free?

It is free to download and play, with ads. Premium removes the ads, gives unlimited hints and endless mosaics, and opens the whole daily archive and Relaxed mode.

Does Cellwise work offline?

Yes. Puzzles, progress and the gallery all live on your device, and no account is needed.

Is every nonogram in Cellwise solvable?

Yes. Each puzzle has exactly one solution, and a solver has confirmed that row and column logic alone reaches it.

What is the difference between a nonogram and griddlers or hanjie?

Nothing. They are different names for the same puzzle, used by different newspapers and publishers.
