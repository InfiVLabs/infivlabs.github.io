Hourplate – Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026. Publisher: InfiV Labs. Contact: infivlabs@gmail.com.

The short version

Hourplate is a fasting timer that works without an account. Your fasts, your plans, your city and your settings are stored only on your phone. We never see them, and they are never sent to us or anyone else.

What stays on your phone

  • The fasts you start, end, edit or delete, with their start and end times and the plan you chose.
  • Your settings: plans, reminders, theme, the city you picked for faith fasts and the calculation method.
  • Days of Ramadan you mark as missed and the fasts you have made up.

Hourplate asks for no location permission: you pick a city from a list built into the app, and sunrise and sunset are worked out on the phone. Android's own backup may copy the app's data to your Google account if you have turned device backup on; that copy is handled by Google under its terms, not by us.

Notifications

Reminders and the "fasting now" notification are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent through a server.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • App analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics): anonymous events such as "a fast was started" or "the paywall was shown", the app version and device model, and crash details. They never include when or how long you fasted, your weight or other health information, or the city you chose: at most the plan's name (such as "16:8") and whether a fast reached its goal.
  • Purchases (Google Play Billing and RevenueCat): when you buy Hourplate Pro, the store handles payment. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt so Pro can be restored on a new phone. We never receive your card details.
  • Ads (Google AdMob), only for free users in some countries: a banner on the Log screen. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy options.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for any purpose other than running the app.

Your choices

  • Export your whole log any time (Log → Export, or Settings → Your data) as CSV or JSON.
  • Delete everything from the phone in Settings → Your data → Delete everything, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Turn reminders off in Settings → Reminders, or in your phone's notification settings.

Health information

Hourplate records when you fast. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Fasting information stays on your phone and is never shared with advertisers or analytics.

Age

Hourplate is meant for adults. The app asks you to confirm you are 18 or older before you start.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
