What breaks a fast? Drinks, calories and medicines in intermittent fasting

What breaks a fast in intermittent fasting: anything with calories. What you can drink, whether coffee, lemon water or gum count, and medicines.

Updated 26 Nov 20264 min read

In intermittent fasting, anything with calories breaks a fast. Water, sparkling water, black coffee and unsweetened tea do not, so you can drink them freely during your fasting hours. Milk, sugar, juice, smoothies and snacks do. Calorie-free sweeteners are a grey area, and prescribed medicines should be taken as your doctor or pharmacist says, fast or no fast.

This page is about intermittent fasting, not religious fasting, and it is not medical advice. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, have diabetes, take regular medicine or have had an eating disorder, talk to a doctor before fasting.

How many calories break a fast?

Strictly, any. A fast is a stretch without food energy, so one calorie technically ends it.

You will often read that "under 50 calories" doesn't count. That is a rule of thumb from the fasting community, not a cut-off that research has tested. What matters more is why you are fasting:

  • For routine and simplicity (no snacking, a fixed eating window): a splash of milk in your morning coffee does not undo the day. Keep it small and consistent.
  • For a clean fast: keep it to water, black coffee and plain tea, and don't count on small amounts "not counting".

Either way, the biggest fast-breakers in practice are drinks people forget about: a milky latte, a sweetened iced tea, a "healthy" smoothie.

Does coffee break a fast?

Black coffee does not. It has almost no calories, and medical guides to intermittent fasting list it among the drinks you can have. Adding milk, cream, sugar, syrup or creamer adds calories and, strictly, ends the fast. If you need something in your coffee, a small splash of milk is the least disruptive choice; sugar and flavoured creamers are the most.

Very strong coffee on an empty stomach upsets some people. If it does, switch to tea or move your coffee into the eating window.

Does lemon water break a fast?

A squeeze of lemon in water adds only a few calories, so it barely breaks a fast in the strict sense and makes no practical difference for most people. A glass of sweetened lemonade or "detox" drink with honey is a different matter: that does break it.

Does gum break a fast?

Regular gum contains sugar, so yes. Sugar-free gum still has a few calories per piece. One piece is unlikely to matter in practice; chewing it all afternoon adds up.

Medicines and supplements

  • Prescribed medicines: keep taking them as prescribed. Some must be taken with food. Never skip a dose or move it to fit a fasting window without asking your doctor or pharmacist.
  • Medicines that lower blood sugar (insulin and some diabetes tablets) can cause dangerous lows during a fast. Don't fast on them without your doctor's plan.
  • Gummy vitamins contain sugar and break a fast. Plain tablets and capsules have little or no energy.
  • Fat-based supplements such as fish oil have a few calories. Many people take them with their first meal instead.

Religious fasts have their own rules

Faith fasts are not intermittent fasting, and their rules are about the faith, not calories. During Ramadan, for example, nothing at all is eaten or drunk from dawn to sunset, water included. Christian and Hindu fasts each have their own customs about what is allowed, which differ between churches, families and communities. Ask your mosque, parish or priest. For Ramadan dates and fasting hours, see Ramadan 2027.

When to stop a fast early

Stop and eat if you feel dizzy, faint, shaky, confused, have palpitations or feel unwell. Fasting should feel uncomfortable at times, but not ill. If this happens often, talk to your doctor and consider a shorter schedule.

Questions

What can I drink while fasting?

Water, sparkling water, black coffee and unsweetened tea, including green and herbal teas. Sugar-free electrolyte drinks are also fine. Anything with sugar, milk or other calories ends the fast.

Does coffee break a fast?

Black coffee does not. Coffee with milk, cream, sugar or syrup does, strictly, because of the added calories.

Does lemon water break a fast?

A squeeze of lemon adds only a few calories, so it barely counts. Sweetened lemon drinks or lemon with honey do break a fast.

How many calories break a fast?

Strictly, any calories end a fast. The popular "under 50 calories" rule is a rule of thumb, not a research-based limit.

Do artificial sweeteners break a fast?

They have no calories, but they may still affect your fasting state, and the evidence is mixed. If you want a clean fast, leave them out.
