Pixel Logic – Nonogram: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Pixel Logic is a puzzle game that runs entirely on your device.

What we collect

  • Nothing that identifies you. The app has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own.
  • Puzzle progress, settings, coins and purchases are stored only on your device. You can export them as a file from Settings and delete them by uninstalling the app.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob shows ads to players who have not bought the one-time unlock. AdMob may use the advertising identifier to serve and measure ads. In the EEA and UK you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before any ad loads, and you can change your choice in Settings. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • RevenueCat processes purchase and subscription receipts so Premium can be restored on a new device and a lapsed subscription is recognised. It receives an anonymous app user id and the store receipt, never your name or email. Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play handle payment.

We use no analytics or crash-reporting SDK in this version.

Children

The app is rated for ages 13 and up and does not knowingly collect data from children.
