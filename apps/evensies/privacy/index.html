Evensies – Group Expense Split: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Evensies records shared costs for a trip, a flat or a night out and works out who owes whom. It runs on your device and moves no money.

What we collect

  • Nothing that identifies you. The app has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own.
  • Groups, names, expenses, amounts and settings are stored only on your device. They leave it only when you send a group yourself: a QR code, a `.evensies` file, a summary image or a PDF/CSV export, through your phone's share sheet to the apps and people you pick. Delete a group in the app, or uninstall the app to delete everything.
  • Camera is used only to scan a group's QR code, on the scan screen, after you allow it. No picture is saved or sent.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob shows ads to people who have not bought Evensies Pro. AdMob may use the advertising identifier to serve and measure ads. In the EEA and UK you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before any ad loads, and you can change your choice in Settings. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics receive anonymous usage events (for example that a group was created) and crash reports, so we can see which features are used and fix crashes. They never receive your group names, people's names or amounts. Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • RevenueCat processes the Evensies Pro purchase receipt so your unlock can be restored on a new device. It receives an anonymous app user id and the store receipt, never your name or email. Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Google Play handles payment.

All of these services send data over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Children

Evensies is meant for adults and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
