Evensies: a split expenses app for trips and shared bills

Split bills with friends. No sign-up, no daily limit, fewest payments to settle

Evensies is a split expenses app for Android. You add what each person paid for a trip, a shared flat or a dinner, and it works out who owes who and the smallest number of payments that makes everyone even. Nobody has to sign up, and there is no daily limit on how many expenses you can add.

What you can do with it

  • Add a cost in a few taps. Type the amount on a big keypad, pick who paid, and save. If two people covered the hotel, add both payers.
  • Split it the way it was really shared. Equally, by exact amounts, by percent, or by shares (the bigger room pays two shares of the rent). Leave someone out of a bill they had no part in.
  • See the fewest payments. The plan draws who pays whom and finds the shortest list of payments that clears every debt. When a payment has happened, tap Mark paid.
  • Check any balance. Every balance opens into a plain sum: what that person paid, their share, and the payments made and received.
  • Send the plan to the group. Share it as an image or as text ready for WhatsApp.
  • Let friends add their own costs. Send the group as a QR code or a small .evensies file. A friend adds their expenses and sends it back, and the two copies merge. There are no accounts and no server.
  • Repeat the regular bills. Rent and utilities can repeat every week or every month.

Who it is for

Evensies suits the person in every group who ends up keeping track: the friend who booked the cabin, the flatmate who pays the internet bill, the couple who share a home but not a bank account.

How it is different

Your groups stay on your phone, with no account and no upload to a server; they leave it only when you share them. Expenses are unlimited for everyone. Free use includes 3 active groups with light ads, and ads never appear while you add an expense. Evensies Pro is one payment, not a subscription: unlimited groups, expenses in other currencies at a rate you type, PDF and spreadsheet export, no ads, and share images without the Evensies line.

Evensies does not move money. It tells you who should pay whom and how much; you pay each other the way you normally do, by cash, bank transfer or a payment app, and then mark it paid.

Questions

Is there a limit on how many expenses I can add?

No. Every user can add as many expenses as they like, in every group. The free version limits you to 3 active groups at a time; archived groups don't count toward that.

Does everyone in the group need the app?

No. One person can keep the whole group and share the plan as an image or a message. Friends who want their own copy can install Evensies, open the group from a QR code or file, add their expenses and send it back to merge.

Can Evensies split rent unevenly?

Yes. Use percent or shares for a split based on income or room size, or type the exact amount for each person, and set the expense to repeat every month.

Does Evensies send or receive payments?

No. It moves no money and connects to no bank. It works out the payments; you make them yourselves and tap Mark paid.
