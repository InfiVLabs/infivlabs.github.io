Privacy policy

Leaftag has no accounts. Your plants, rooms, photos, care history and light readings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them.

Photos you add are copied into the app’s private storage on your phone. The camera is also used, only while you measure, to estimate light from exposure settings; no image is saved or sent.

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

If you buy Leaftag Plus, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google and verified through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt. It never receives your plant data.

In India, Brazil, Mexico and Indonesia the free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the “Find your plant” screen and, only if you choose it, a short video that unlocks an extra plant slot or more light readings. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, an approximate location derived from your IP address and how you interact with ads (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Where the law requires consent you are asked first, and you can change your choice any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. Leaftag Plus removes the ads. Your plant data is never shared with advertisers.

Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics receive app usage events such as “plant watered” (counts and task types, never plant names, notes or photos), an app-instance ID, the device model and OS version, an approximate location from the IP address and crash reports, so we can see which features are used and fix crashes. This data is never used for ads. In the EEA and the UK they collect nothing unless you have agreed in Google’s consent form.

Export or delete: Settings → Export backup gives you all your data as a file; deleting the app deletes everything.
