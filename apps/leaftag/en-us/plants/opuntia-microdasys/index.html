How often to water Bunny ears cactus

In a 6-inch (15 cm) plastic pot in bright, indirect light, Bunny ears cactus usually needs water about every 21 days while it grows and about every 47 days in midwinter around 41° north, the latitude of New York and Chicago (further south, winter changes less; in Canada, a little more). Pot and spot change that:

PotSpring–summerFall–winter
5 in (12 cm), plastic18 days40 days
6 in (15 cm), terracotta17 days38 days
10 in (25 cm), plastic30 days60 days

These are starting points, not rules: push a finger or a wooden skewer deep into the soil before you water. Water only when it is dry all the way down; any dampness means wait.

Light: where to put Bunny ears cactus

Best in direct light; it copes with bright, indirect. As a rough guide, measured at midday: low is under 1,100 lux (about 100 foot-candles), medium 1,100–5,400 lux (100–500 fc), bright indirect 5,400–10,800 lux (500–1,000 fc) and direct sun above that. A phone reading is approximate.

Is Bunny ears cactus toxic to cats and dogs?

It is listed as non-toxic to cats and dogs.

If a pet has eaten part of a plant, call your vet or the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center at (888) 426-4435 (a fee may apply). This page is general information, not veterinary advice.

Sources: ASPCA: are succulents safe have around pets, ASPCA: tree cactus

Feeding and repotting

Feed about every 42 days from spring to early fall, less often as the days shorten, and not at all in midwinter. Use the fertilizer at a quarter of the strength on the label: its roots burn easily.

Humidity and temperature

Dry indoor air is fine. Comfortable between 41 °F (5 °C) and 95 °F (35 °C).

Tips

  • Water only when the soil is bone dry, and hardly at all in winter.
  • Its tiny glochids lodge in skin: handle it with tongs or folded paper.
