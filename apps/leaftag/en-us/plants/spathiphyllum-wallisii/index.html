How often to water Peace lily

In a 6-inch (15 cm) plastic pot in bright, indirect light, Peace lily usually needs water about every 5 days while it grows and about every 7 days in midwinter around 41° north, the latitude of New York and Chicago (further south, winter changes less; in Canada, a little more). Pot and spot change that:

PotSpring–summerFall–winter
5 in (12 cm), plastic4 days6 days
6 in (15 cm), terracotta4 days6 days
10 in (25 cm), plastic7 days10 days

These are starting points, not rules: touch the soil before you water. Water as soon as the surface feels dry; this plant should never dry out further down.

Light: where to put Peace lily

Best in bright, indirect light; it copes with low. As a rough guide, measured at midday: low is under 1,100 lux (about 100 foot-candles), medium 1,100–5,400 lux (100–500 fc), bright indirect 5,400–10,800 lux (500–1,000 fc) and direct sun above that. A phone reading is approximate.

Is Peace lily toxic to cats and dogs?

Treat it as toxic to cats and dogs: keep it out of reach.

If a pet has eaten part of a plant, call your vet or the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center at (888) 426-4435 (a fee may apply). This page is general information, not veterinary advice.

Feeding and repotting

Feed about every 21 days from spring to early fall, less often as the days shorten, and not at all in midwinter. Use the fertilizer at half the strength on the label.

Humidity and temperature

Average room humidity suits it. Comfortable between 61 °F (16 °C) and 86 °F (30 °C).

Tips

  • Check the soil every few days; water when the top 2 cm is dry.
  • It droops dramatically when thirsty but perks up within hours of a drink.
